title: "Character Creation"
---

The rules for creating a character are the same as in base D&D 5th edition. You've had some limited experience at this point and developed some skills. Your characters begin with 900 XP, at level 3.

Create your character in Foundry VTT using its character builder. The custom content for this campaign, such as the Cyberpunk backgrounds, weapons, armor, and gear, is provided through my Foundry module.

## Character Rules

* **Advancement:** Experience points (XP).
* **Hit Points:** Fixed. When you gain a level, take the fixed hit point value for your class instead of rolling.
* **Encumbrance:** The standard encumbrance rules are used. [[Currency|Cryptopoints]] have no weight.
* **Optional Class Features:** Allowed.
* **Customizing Your Origin:** Allowed (*Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*).
* **Prerequisites:** Feat prerequisites and multiclassing ability score requirements apply.

## Sources

This campaign uses the **2014 rules** of 5th edition. Content from the 2024 rules is not used.

Character options may come from the following official books:

| Book | Races | Classes and Subclasses |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| *Player's Handbook* | Yes | Yes |
| *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide* | Yes | Yes |
| *Xanathar's Guide to Everything* | - | Yes |
| *Guildmaster's Guide to Ravnica* | Yes | Yes |
| *Mythic Odysseys of Theros* | Yes | Yes |
| *Eberron: Rising from the Last War* | Yes | Yes |
| *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything* | No Custom Lineage | Yes |
| *Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft* | Yes | Yes |
| *Strixhaven: A Curriculum of Chaos* | Yes | - |
| *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons* | Yes | Yes |
| *Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse* | Yes | - |
| *Dragonlance: Shadow of the Dragon Queen* | No | Lunar Sorcery only |

Feats and spells may also come from any of these books, except *Dragonlance: Shadow of the Dragon Queen*. The Strixhaven Initiate and Strixhaven Mascot feats are not allowed, and the Gunner feat is replaced by [[Feats#Gunslinger|Gunslinger]].

## Race Selection

You may select any race, subrace, or lineage from the books above, except the **Custom Lineage**. If a race appears in more than one book, use its version from *Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse* when one exists. You may use the Customizing Your Origin rules.

Every race has a place in [[Zeal City]]. There are some lore-specific additions to some of the races, which I have outlined in [[Demographics]].

## Class Selection

All classes and subclasses from the books above are free to choose from, and you may use any and all optional features for the classes. If a class or subclass appears in more than one book, use the most recent version (for example, the Artificer from *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything* rather than *Eberron: Rising from the Last War*). Multiclassing is allowed as well.

## Ability Scores

Use Point Buy for your ability score generation.

## Backgrounds

You must select one of the custom Cyberpunk Backgrounds. I've tried to make them all fit with a Cyberpunk setting while keeping them in-line with existing 5e backgrounds.

It is important to note that, regardless of your background, **you were about to end up on the streets, having no choice but to start looking for lancer work**. Take this into account when you write your backstory, since exactly what has put you into this situation may come up during the campaign.

You may choose any of the backgrounds listed in [[System Updates/Backgrounds/index|Backgrounds]]. Each background's page lists its proficiencies, languages, starting equipment, and starting cp.

## Religion

If you choose to follow a religion, please refer to [[Religion]]. If you are playing a **Cleric**, choose the deity for your Cleric domain.
