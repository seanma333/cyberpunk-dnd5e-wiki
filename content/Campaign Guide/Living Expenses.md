While we normally ignore this in D&D campaigns, daily living expenses are a big part of living in a Cyberpunk world. [[Zeal City]] is split into 10 different districts, each of which has a different **average lifestyle cost**. At the end of each day, you must pay for a lifestyle cost within one step of the district's lifestyle cost. This covers getting a room, as well as food and lifestyle expenses. If you do not pay these expenses, you will be sleeping on the street in Wretched conditions, and you are very likely to be robbed in your sleep (if in a poorer district) or be kicked out by police (if in a richer district).

| [[Azorius District\|Azorius]] | Administration | Comfortable (200 cp/day) |

## Resting

You may attempt a long rest once per day as usual, but based on your lifestyle, it may be more difficult to get the full benefits of the long rest. When you attempt to take a long rest, if the associated lifestyle cost of the area you are in has a **Rest DC**, as outlined in the table below, roll a d20. If your result meets or exceeds the Rest DC, you successfully take a long rest. Otherwise, you only gain the benefits of a short rest, *but you do not need to roll for exhaustion*. You do not need to roll if there is no Rest DC, and you get a long rest as per normal rules.

Alternatively, you may choose to spend a number of **downtime days** with a certain lifestyle to gain the benefits of a long rest, so long as the days are spent in a **safe location**.The required number of downtime days for a lifestyle is outlined in the table below. You can not take any missions or dangerous activities during these downtime days. However, you may spend this time doing other roleplaying activities, such as researching, crafting, or making social connections.

| Lifestyle | Rest DC | Downtime Days | Additional Benefits |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Wretched | 19 | 10 | - |
| Squalid | 17 | 5 | - |
| Poor | 15 | 4 | - |
| Modest | 13 | 3 | - |
| Comfortable | 11 | 2 | - |
| Wealthy | - | 1 | - |
| Aristocratic | - | 1 | Regain all hit die, rather than half. |
