---
title: "Starting Equipment"
---

Because of all the custom items and equipment for this campaign, we will be using a version of **gold buy** for your starting equipment, rather than class equipment. **When you create your character, *do not take your class's starting equipment or gold*.** Instead, everyone begins with **a smartphone,** **25,000 cp, a backpack**, as well as the equipment and cryptostick (with its cp) listed in your [[System Updates/Backgrounds/index|background]]. This represents your entire life savings up to this point. Before the campaign begins, you may spend this on any weapons, armor, tools, or equipment that you may need.

Keep in mind that you will likely want to keep some money on you for daily [[Rest and Recovery|living expenses]].

## Starting Cyberware

During character creation, you may start with **one Tier 1 cyberware** and have it installed for your character without any cost. Tier 1 cyberware are listed below. Full cyberware descriptions are in [[System Updates/Cyberware/index|Cyberware]].

| Name | Summary |
| :---- | :---- |
| [[Armblade]] | Replace arm with a prosthetic arm with grafted weapon |
| [[Cyberclaws]] | Gain claws that can extend from your fingers or hands |
| [[Encephalon]] | Rewire brain connections, boosting INT checks and saves. |
| [[Hydraulic Jacks]] | Legs are augmented with hydraulics, enabling faster movement |
| [[Immune System Booster]] | Nanomachines boost immune system against disease and poison |
| [[Integrated Grapple Gun]] | Gain a grappling hook launcher in your arm |
| [[Muscle Reinforcement]] | Enhanced muscles and strength, boosting STR checks and saves |
| [[Sensory Enhancement]] | Gain darkvision 120 ft or improve existing darkvision by 90 ft. |
| [[Smartgun Augment]] | Allows you to interface with [[Weapons#Firearms\|firearms]] that have a [[Weapon Upgrades#Smartgun System\|Smartgun System]] installed |
| [[Subdermal Plating]] | Fuse armor plating to your skin, boosting AC and physical saves |
