## Beginnings

![[Zeal City Street.png]]

*[[Zeal City]] has never been kind to those on the streets. You've known this your entire life. Doesn't matter how special you are or how skilled you are, the only real "gift" in this day and age is cold hard cash. Now, for one reason or another, you're running low on it. And you are about to be tossed into those very streets. Whatever happened in your past is your business, but there's little hope for your future if you don't get some crips flowing into your wallet.*

*That's why, when you found yourself in this shit situation, you took up some under-the-table lancing jobs. A couple of paydays could at least delay the inevitable. And you found that for whatever reason, you've got some skills. Maybe you met some kindred spirits at those jobs. But, you know that lancers are paid by the job, and there's only so many jobs out there that a lancer can get on their own with no real connections. You know if you want to survive long-term, you're going to need a fixer: someone well-connected who can get you some clients on the reg.*

*You look out at the neon city lights as you ride the city metro, a red envelope tucked into your pocket. Someone has invited you to meet with them at a nightclub in the [[Rakdos District|Rakdos district]], offering to be a fixer for an up-and-coming Lancer like yourself. This may be your one chance to secure your future.*

## Setting Notes

This campaign takes place in an original setting heavily inspired by the cyberpunk genre, as well as both the Ravnica and Eberron campaign settings. In a world called **Ryse**, classic D&D magic and modern-to-futuristic technology coexist. The primary location for this campaign is **Zeal City**, a sprawling and crowded neon metropolis populated by all of the D&D races, and one of the few places in the world that survived the fallout of a massive war which decimated much of the arable land in the world. While it does have its own government, the city is in reality run by 13 megacorporations who control everything from politics to law enforcement to military.

Player characters take the role of **Lancers** (short for freelancers). Like the adventurers of old, Lancers are highly skilled individuals who work for whomever they choose, using their special skills and abilities to perform dangerous jobs for anyone from the lowly street gangs to high-level corporate executives, and get paid for their success. These jobs are normally arranged by **Fixers**, who organize connections with clients. While many people see Lancers as criminals or mercenaries, many others see them as the only ones with any actual freedom in a cruel and unjust world...

For more details about the setting and history of Zeal City, refer to [[Setting and Lore/index|Setting and Lore]].
