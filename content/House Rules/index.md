These house rules change or add to the standard D&D 5th Edition (2014) rules for this campaign.

## Power Attacks

**Sharpshooter is banned in this campaign**. This is due to the prevalence of ranged combat in this setting, and the first two benefits of Sharpshooter removes two major aspects of tactical ranged combat in D&D: long range and cover. However, its third benefit, trading accuracy for extra damage, is fine on its own, so to keep that option available to everyone, I am implementing the following house rule instead. *This does not require a feat, and it works with melee and ranged weapon attacks alike.*

**Before you make an attack with an unarmed strike or a weapon that you are proficient with, you may choose to take a penalty to the attack roll equal to your proficiency bonus. If that attack hits, you add twice your proficiency bonus to the damage roll.**

## Attacked while Flying

Flying races are not restricted in this campaign. However, I will be using the following house rule: **If a creature that is flying takes damage from an attack, it must make a Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. The DC equals 10 or half the damage it takes, whichever number is higher.** This works like a concentration check, but uses Strength instead of Constitution. A creature only makes this saving throw the first time it takes damage from an attack each turn.

Keep in mind that when a creature is knocked prone while flying, it falls unless it has the ability to hover or it is being held aloft by magic.
