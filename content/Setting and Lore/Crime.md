While the rich and powerful live in their towers high above the ground, the streets of [[Zeal City]] are far less glamorous. Crime of all types is a common sight in the city streets, from lowly street criminals to large crime syndicates. For most people, their best outcome is to not become a target for criminals, but whether it's being robbed on the street by a pickpocket or being caught in the crossfire of a gang war, many could only be so lucky.

## Gangs

Numerous gangs exist in the streets of Zeal City. While they are most common in poor or squalid districts, their presence can still be felt in the rest of the city, particularly the noisy biker gangs. Most street gangs are small, existing as a "family" unit for the underprivileged who turn to crime as a way to survive in this world.

Many street gangs take the names of classic D&D monsters, e.g. The Owlbears, The Manticores, etc. Most such gangs commit street-level crimes like robbery, but some branch out into other fields. One gang, the Mimics, mainly consisted of hackers who perform identity theft and white-collar crime.

## Organized Crime

While numerous gangs roam the streets, the truly powerful crime groups exist under the nose of the law. In the criminal underworld, large organized crime syndicates run black markets operations from the shadows. Their size and influence in the underworld are so large, they can almost rival megacorporations. While different organizations get along with (or at least tolerate) each other, conflicts will occasionally arise, leading to all-out war between the syndicates on the streets of the city.

Most crime groups are based on familial bonds or loyalty structures. Some, like drug cartels, specialize in providing certain illegal products. Others dip their hands into all sorts of businesses, including gambling, theft, trafficking, organ harvesting, and other criminal enterprises. Protection rackets are also a common source of income for the organizations, which double as a way for the syndicates to establish their territory in areas where law enforcement is either scarce, corrupt, or both.
