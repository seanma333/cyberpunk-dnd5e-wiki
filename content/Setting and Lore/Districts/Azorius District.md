The Azorius District (named after the human paladin Azorius the Unwavering) is the Administrative district of [[Zeal City]]. It is the location of Zeal Tower (the seat of government for the city), the Arbiter's Courthouse, and Lawmage Plaza. Although it is small in area, it is one of the most densely populated districts.

## What It's Like

Azorius is all queues, checkpoints, and people trying to get somewhere on time. Government buildings crowd narrow streets beneath Zeal Tower, while court clerks, advocates, municipal workers, and petitioners fill the cafés and transit stops. Public plazas are kept orderly and well lit; cameras and uniformed security make it hard to forget that most of the city's rules are written here.

For residents, the district is convenient and expensive. A permit, hearing, or appeal can take a day—or a month—depending on whose desk it reaches. People come here to change their name, challenge a fine, register a business, or plead their case. Everyone has a reason to be in Azorius, and almost everyone is waiting for someone else's decision.

## Landmarks

- **Zeal Tower** is the seat of the city government and the most visible symbol of Azorius authority. The Executive Council works from here, making the tower a destination for officials, lobbyists, petitioners, and anyone hoping to influence city policy.
- **The Arbiter's Courthouse** handles the city's major legal proceedings. Its halls see everyone from ordinary residents contesting a ruling to corporate representatives fighting over contracts and jurisdiction.
- **Lawmage Plaza** is the Medani Security Group's headquarters and public face in Zeal City. The plaza's polished grounds and uniformed lawmages make it one of the district's most carefully watched spaces.
