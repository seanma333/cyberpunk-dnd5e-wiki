The Boros District (named after the aasimar fighter Aurelia Boros) is the location of nearly all of the city's army and naval bases, as well as multiple arms manufacturing facilities. Corporations that primarily work with military contracts and development in [[Zeal City]] have their major presence in Boros.

## What It's Like

Boros is a district of barracks, parade grounds, secured factories, and transport yards. The streets are broad enough for armored convoys, and the steady churn of drills, shift changes, and deliveries gives the area a regimented rhythm. Military insignia and corporate logos share the same walls; from a distance, it can be difficult to tell where the city's forces end and a contractor's begin.

Uniformed personnel are a familiar sight, from career soldiers and naval crews to factory workers building their equipment. Access rules vary block by block, and a wrong turn can bring you to a gate or inspection point. For civilians, Boros offers reliable work and a strong security presence, but also reminders that the city is always preparing for its next conflict.

## Landmarks

- **The military and naval bases** are the district's defining landmarks: fenced compounds, training grounds, and docks where the city's forces prepare for threats beyond the peninsula.
- **The arms manufacturing quarter** is a landscape of guarded factories and corporate facilities. Deneith Armaments has a major presence here, and its Armaknight training grounds are a familiar sight to anyone passing through the district.

### Neighborhood Spot

- **The Old Church Bar** is a neighborhood bar converted from a former church of the Warrior. It is a modest gathering place for workers and security staff, far from the district's secured compounds.
