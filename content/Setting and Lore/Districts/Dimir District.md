The Dimir District (named after the halfling rogue, Szadek Dimir) was originally the major connection of Zeal to the outside, where news and information would come in and out of the city. Nowadays, it is the major tech hub of the city, being the location of multiple major software companies, including the HQ of the megacorps [[Sivis Communications]] and [[Thuranni Media Technologies]]. As such, some of the richest people in the city live in the skyscrapers of Dimir. The City Intelligence Service (CIS) also has its headquarters in Dimir, its operatives running counter-intelligence on threats within and outside the city limits.

## What It's Like

Dimir's towers are stitched together by fiber lines, elevated walkways, and the constant glow of screens. Corporate campuses sit beside apartment blocks, co-working floors, server farms, and studios producing the feeds that reach every corner of the city. Information is the district's main export, and even the public spaces feel watched, measured, and quietly curated.

The skyline is wealthy, but the people who keep it running work around the clock: technicians, moderators, couriers, analysts, and night-shift cleaners. A person can disappear into Dimir's crowds—or leave a trail in every system they touch. Residents learn to ask who is listening before they speak, and to treat a leaked message as carefully as a loaded weapon.
