The Golgari District (named after the dark elf druid, Gol'gar) is primarily the agricultural district of the city, growing most of the food supply and having the largest fishing port in Zeal. However, after the population of the city exploded, the district has extended its operations underground. Now, it also maintains the city's sewage and waste systems, as well as growing (mostly synthetic) food for a hungry city in the dark tunnels below the surface. The Vadalis Arcology, a giant building that is the headquarters of [[Vadalis Consolidated]], is located in Golgari.

## What It's Like

Golgari smells of wet soil, fish, and the sharp chemical tang of food processing. Its working waterfront is busy before dawn, while greenhouses and cultivation halls feed the city above and below ground. Beneath the streets, service tunnels carry water, waste, workers, and crops through a world most residents never see. The Vadalis Arcology rises over it all, a corporate landmark amid the district's practical sprawl.

People here keep Zeal fed and functioning: dock crews, growers, sanitation workers, mechanics, and the families who live close to their shifts. Fresh produce and actual meat are coveted luxuries; much of the city's food is grown in controlled environments or manufactured at scale. The district's underground spaces are vital, but they are easy to get lost in—and not every tunnel is as clean, safe, or well mapped as the city claims.

## Landmarks

- **Vadalis Arcology** is a vast, self-sustaining vertical complex and the headquarters of Vadalis Consolidated. Its living quarters, research spaces, and food-production systems make it feel like a company town built inside a single tower.
- **The fishing port and waterfront** are among the busiest parts of Golgari. Fishing vessels, food shipments, and dockworkers keep the city's largest harbor in motion from before dawn.
- **The underground farms** extend Golgari's food production below the surface. Their greenhouses and vertical farms share space with the tunnels and utility systems that carry the district's waste and water.
