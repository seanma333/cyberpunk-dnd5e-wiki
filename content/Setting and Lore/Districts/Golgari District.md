The Golgari District (named after the dark elf druid, Gol'gar) is primarily the agricultural district of the city, growing most of the food supply and having the largest fishing port in Zeal. However, after the population of the city exploded, the district has extended its operations underground. Now, it also maintains the city's sewage and waste systems, as well as growing (mostly synthetic) food for a hungry city in the dark tunnels below the surface. The Vadalis Arcology, a giant building that is the headquarters of [[Vadalis Consolidated]], is located in Golgari.
