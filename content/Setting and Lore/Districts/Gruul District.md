The Gruul District (named after the orc barbarian, Gruul the Mighty), was once the area in the Zeal Peninsula used for mining Residuum, for its proximity to the caverns. For some time, it was heavily developed as the mines were the most profitable areas of [[Zeal City]]. However, over time, the air pollution created by the massive mining operations made the district difficult to live in. The mining operators turned to using [[Demographics#Warforged|Warforged]] and automatons for their mining purposes, as they did not need to breathe in the polluted air, but the district's economy was barely surviving. Soon, the area became all but abandoned by the city. The mines themselves are heavily guarded, but the district is a mere shadow of what it once was. The dirty streets are now home to anarchist crime gangs, who wreak havoc all around the city, as well as run down slums occupied by the truly downtrodden.
