The Izzet District (named after the gnome artificer, Izzet the Wondrous) is the city's industrial and manufacturing district, as well as the location of the city's major power plants. Factories and warehouses are the main features of this district, and much of the city's infrastructure is produced here. Despite the heavy corporate presence, the living conditions in the district are quite poor due to the noise and pollution caused by the factories.

## What It's Like

Izzet announces itself with noise: turbines whining, freight engines clattering, warning bells, and the thump of machinery running through the night. Power stations and factory stacks dominate the skyline. Trucks and cargo trams squeeze past warehouses, loading bays, and worker housing built close to the shifts that keep the plants running. Even the air seems to vibrate.

The district makes the things Zeal depends on, from building materials to the components that keep its lights on. Factory workers, engineers, repair crews, and shift vendors fill its streets at odd hours. Some blocks are modern and tightly controlled; others bear the wear of generations of industrial use. A breakdown here can affect the whole city, but the people who live beside the infrastructure rarely get to decide how it is run.

## Landmarks

- **The power plants and factory complexes** define Izzet's skyline and keep the city's infrastructure running. Many are owned or operated by corporations, with access restricted to workers and authorized visitors.
- **Deadspace** is a hidden bar for lancers, located beneath the abandoned Continent Hotel. Run by retired lancers, it offers a rare neutral ground where crews can meet and trade information. Its central rule is simple: no killing inside Deadspace. Invite-only back rooms are shielded against wireless signals and use secure wired lines for private calls.
