The Izzet District (named after the gnome artificer, Izzet the Wondrous) is the city's industrial and manufacturing district, as well as the location of the city's major power plants. Factories and warehouses are the main features of this district, and much of the city's infrastructure is produced here. Despite the heavy corporate presence, the living conditions in the district are quite poor due to the noise and pollution caused by the factories.
