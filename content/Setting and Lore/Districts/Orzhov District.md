The Orzhov District (named after the dwarven cleric Father Orzhova) is the city's business district. As the site of the city's main churches, it has been a popular gathering place for business transactions since before urbanization. Now, tall skyscrapers covered in bright neon advertisements fill the skyline, while the hustle of workers entering and leaving their shifts are constant throughout the day. The offices of many corporations, including several megacorporations, are found in Orzhov.

The bridge crossing leads to the Luxury Islands, where multiple business hotels and resorts are located, as well as the city's major airport and teleportation hub. Expensive restaurants, shopping, and clubs line every street of the islands, making them a popular tourist area.

## What It's Like

Orzhov is where Zeal City puts its wealth on display. Office towers, old churches, polished plazas, and neon façades compete for attention; security staff and private vehicles keep the business streets moving. During shift changes, workers pour between skyscrapers. After dark, the best restaurants and clubs fill with executives, visitors, and people hoping to be seen by the right person.

Across the bridge, the Luxury Islands feel like a different city: hotels, resorts, airport terminals, and a teleportation hub designed to make arrivals smooth and departures effortless. The Koi Hotel is in Orzhov, a neutral meeting place for the Five Houses. In a district where money can buy comfort, access, and discretion, the real question is often who is paying—and what they expect in return.

## Landmarks

- **The Luxury Islands** are Orzhov's best-known destination for visitors, with resorts, business hotels, expensive shopping, and private clubs gathered around the city's main travel hubs.
- **The airport and teleportation hub** handle the city's most important arrivals and departures. Their terminals are built for speed and discretion, serving corporate travelers as well as tourists.
- **The Koi Hotel** is a high-end hotel and neutral meeting place for the Five Houses. Its executive rooms offer privacy for delicate negotiations, while the hotel's public face blends into Orzhov's luxury hospitality scene.
- **The great churches of Orzhov** are among the oldest civic landmarks in the district. They predate the modern business skyline and remain active places of worship and public gathering.
