The Orzhov District (named after the dwarven cleric Father Orzhova) is the city's business district. As the site of the city's main churches, it has been a popular gathering place for business transactions since before urbanization. Now, tall skyscrapers covered in bright neon advertisements fill the skyline, while the hustle of workers entering and leaving their shifts are constant throughout the day. The offices of many corporations, including several megacorporations, are found in Orzhov.

The bridge crossing leads to the Luxury Islands, where multiple business hotels and resorts are located, as well as the city's major airport and teleportation hub. Expensive restaurants, shopping, and clubs line every street of the islands, making them a popular tourist area.
