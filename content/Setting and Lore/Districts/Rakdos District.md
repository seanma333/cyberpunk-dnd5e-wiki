The Rakdos District (named after the tiefling bard Rakdos) is the city's main entertainment district. While the [[Orzhov District|Orzhov district]]'s entertainment caters to the wealthy, the Rakdos nightclubs, casinos, and bars are for people from all walks of life. The area lacks the glitz of the rich neighborhoods of Orzhov, but the nights are bustling with activity. Although it does have a law enforcement presence, the Rakdos district is primarily run by several criminal organizations that enforce their own laws, which the city does little to interfere with. These are the [[Setting and Lore/The Five Houses/index|Five Houses]]: [[The Lazuli Mafia]], [[The Black Lotus Triad]], [[The Rimewind Bratva]], [[The Kurogane Yakuza]], and [[The Midsummer Crew]].

## What It's Like

Rakdos wakes up at night. Neon signs spill over crowded sidewalks, music leaks from clubs, and food stalls serve people coming off shift alongside gamblers, performers, and visitors. The venues range from cheap bars and basement shows to polished casinos, each with its own crowd and door policy. There is glamour here, but it belongs to the people making the night happen as much as to those spending money in it.

The Five Houses maintain an uneasy order across their territories. Their influence is easy to notice in who gets welcomed, who gets warned, and which disputes are handled quietly. Ordinary residents still go to work, meet friends, and raise families here; they simply learn which streets belong to whom and when to keep walking. The police are present, but locals know that calling them is only one way to ask for help—and rarely the quickest.
