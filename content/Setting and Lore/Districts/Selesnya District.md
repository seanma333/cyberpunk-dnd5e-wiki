The Selesnya District (named after the wood elf ranger Silvana Selesnya) is the city's nature district. Situated on a large island, Selesnya is fairly isolated from the rest of the city. Because of this, Selesnya has managed to keep much of its natural environment relatively undisturbed, with much of the land being used for wildlife preserves. The city parks department is located in Selesnya as well, and maintains smaller parks throughout the city as small places of refuge from the urban sprawl. Because actual meat is so rare and expensive, Selesnya has had problems with poachers over the years, killing the wildlife and selling their exotic meat on the black market. It is also the site of a Fey Crossing, which opened 50 years before. It is now closed and heavily guarded.
