The Simic District (named after the high elf wizard Vannifar Simic) is the city's university district. The district is the home of the main campus for Zeal University, a prestigious college for students around the world. The university's medical research is funded by [[Jorasco Biotechnology]], who also have multiple facilities throughout the district.

## What It's Like

Simic is shaped by the university: lecture halls, laboratories, student housing, libraries, clinics, and cafés spill across a busy campus district. Students and researchers come from across Ryse, bringing ambition, rivalries, and a steady demand for cheap meals and late-night study space. The campus is prestigious, but the neighborhood around it has all the mess of a place growing faster than its rents can keep up.

Jorasco's research facilities and medical centers are part of daily life here. Some are open clinics; others are controlled campuses with strict access and polished corporate branding. New treatments and discoveries promise better lives, while questions about consent, cost, and who benefits never stay far from the conversation. In Simic, a class project, a clinical trial, and a corporate breakthrough can be separated by only a few streets.

## Landmarks

- **Zeal University** is the district's defining institution, drawing students and scholars from across Ryse. Its main campus brings lecture halls, libraries, laboratories, and student life together in one busy neighborhood.
- **Jorasco's medical and research facilities** are spread across Simic. The corporation funds university medical research and operates clinics and laboratories nearby, making healthcare and biotechnology a visible part of the district's daily life.
