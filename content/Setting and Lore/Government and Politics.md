---
title: "Government and Politics"
---

The government of [[Zeal City]] is separated into three branches: The Executive Council, the Legislative Parliament, and the Judiciary Court. Officially, the government holds the major political power in the city, but in reality, every branch is controlled by the megacorporations, making the official government a political figurehead at best.

* The Executive Council consists of representatives from each of the megacorporations. While the Council Leader is officially elected by the people, their decisions are ultimately controlled by the other members.  
* The Legislative Parliament officially is represented by the leaders of each of the city municipalities, but ultimately making contracts with the megacorporations drives their decisions.  
* The Judiciary Court is officially appointed by the Executive Council.
