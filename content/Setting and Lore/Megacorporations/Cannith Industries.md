*"If it moves, we made it move."*

## Overview
Cannith Industries (CID) is a legendary megacorporation in Ryse, widely regarded as the oldest and most influential corporate entity still in operation. Founded during the onset of the Industrial Revolution, the company has played a central role in shaping the modern technological landscape. From the earliest steam-driven machines to today's advanced cybernetic lifeforms, Cannith's fingerprints are found on nearly every piece of metal machinery.

## Core Industries
Cannith is the global leader in the design and manufacture of mechanical components, particularly for vehicles, robotics, and cybernetic systems. Its industrial catalog spans from heavy-duty engine parts and hydraulic systems to intricate micro-actuators and synthetic muscle fibers. It is said that if something contains a motor, servo, or steel joint, there’s a good chance Cannith made it—or the tools that built it.

## Robotics & the Warforged
The company's robotics division is among the most advanced in the world. Its crowning achievement is the creation of the **[[Demographics#Warforged|Warforged]]**, a line of sapient humanoid constructs originally developed for combat purposes. These autonomous machines gained significant popularity during and after the Mana Wars, with some now integrated into society as workers, bodyguards, and even civil servants. The ethical and social implications of their existence remain a hot topic of public debate.

## Cybernetics Division
Cannith's foray into cybernetics began during the brutal Mana Wars, when the need for replacement limbs and life-preserving augmentations skyrocketed. Since then, Cannith has grown to dominate the cybernetics market, offering everything from prosthetic limbs and neural implants to full-body reconstruction. Their cyberware is known for its reliability, durability, and seamless integration with human biology.

## Leadership: Starrin Cannith
Starrin Cannith, the enigmatic and nearly mythic CEO, has helmed the company for nearly two centuries. A human by birth, Starrin has extended his life far beyond natural limits by continually replacing his organic components with cybernetic ones. Rumors suggest that less than 5% of his original body remains. He assumed control of the company as a young man during the early days of the Mana Wars and has led it with a mix of visionary brilliance and ruthless pragmatism.

## Presence in Zeal City
In [[Zeal City]], Cannith Industries maintains a strong corporate presence, primarily centered in the **[[Izzet District]]**, an area known for its industrial energy and engineering innovation. The company also operates state-of-the-art research facilities in the **[[Simic District]]**, focusing on the next generation of cybernetic and biomechanical enhancements.

## Reputation & Influence
Feared by its rivals and respected by engineers worldwide, Cannith Industries is both a symbol of technological progress and a reminder of corporate omnipresence. Its role in shaping both the battlefield and the body has made it indispensable to modern society—and potentially dangerous in equal measure.
