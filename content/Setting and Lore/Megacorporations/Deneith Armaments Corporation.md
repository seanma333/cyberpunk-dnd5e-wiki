*"Peace through superior firepower."*

## Overview
Deneith Armaments Corporation (DAC) is the largest and most formidable weapons manufacturer in the world, renowned for its high-powered firearms, advanced combat armor, and cutting-edge anti-mage technology. With a legacy forged in the crucible of the Mana Wars, Deneith has become a cornerstone of modern military and security infrastructure, supplying everyone from national armies to elite corporate enforcers.

## Weapons & Armor Manufacturing
Deneith’s primary industry lies in the mass production of conventional and high-caliber firearms. Its product line ranges from compact sidearms to heavy-duty assault weapons, favored by soldiers, law enforcement, and corporate security teams alike. The company also specializes in the design and production of **heavy armor**, optimized for urban warfare and magical resistance, making it the standard for many elite response units around the globe.

## Anti-Mage Technology
One of Deneith's most defining innovations is its **anti-mage weaponry**. Pioneered by company founder **Breven Deneith**, these weapons were instrumental in turning the tide during the later stages of the Mana Wars. Utilizing specialized ammunition and arcane-disruption tech, Deneith's anti-magic armaments remain unmatched, earning the corporation near-total control over this critical weapons niche.

## The Armaknights
DAC’s reach extends beyond manufacturing into paramilitary operations through its elite private security division, the **Armaknights**. These armored operatives are deployed for high-threat engagements, corporate protection, and battlefield support, always armed with Deneith’s most advanced weaponry. Notably, many Armaknights are former soldiers or enhanced operatives, making them highly trained and utterly loyal.

## Leadership: Talon Silverfang
At the helm of Deneith Armaments is **Talon Silverfang**, a dragonborn with a decorated military background. Silverfang previously led the Armaknight division before ascending to CEO, bringing a warrior's precision and discipline to the corporation's strategic vision. His leadership style emphasizes strength, efficiency, and relentless innovation.

## Presence in Zeal City
In **[[Zeal City]]**, Deneith maintains a stronghold in the **[[Boros District]]**, an area known for its militarized infrastructure and security operations. Here, the company operates advanced manufacturing plants and rigorous training academies for Armaknights and affiliated forces.

## Reputation & Influence
Feared by insurgents and respected by generals, Deneith Armaments Corporation is a symbol of unyielding power. Critics often accuse the company of escalating conflicts and profiting from perpetual war, but few deny its role in stabilizing volatile zones and protecting high-value interests. Where there is conflict, there is Deneith—armed, armored, and ready.
