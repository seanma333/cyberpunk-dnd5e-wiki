*"Luxury is our legacy."*

## Overview
Ghallanda Hospitality Group (GHG) is the world’s foremost provider of mid-range to luxury accommodations and holds near-total dominance over the global hotel and high-end lodging industry. Known for its impeccable service standards and opulent properties, Ghallanda’s brand has become synonymous with comfort, status, and exclusivity. A household name in the luxury travel and corporate housing sectors, Ghallanda’s influence extends far beyond the hospitality industry.

## Hospitality Empire
The Ghallanda brand umbrella encompasses hundreds of hotel chains and boutique lodging experiences across the globe. While many of these were once independent hospitality brands, they have been systematically acquired and absorbed into Ghallanda’s vast corporate network. Today, any hotel bearing the **Ghallanda** name is widely understood to represent high-quality service, exquisite design, and premium comfort.

## Real Estate Development
Though best known for its hotels, Ghallanda is also the **largest real estate developer** among the thirteen megacorporations. Originally focused on **corporate real estate**—such as business towers and executive residences—Ghallanda has since expanded into **residential land development**, securing long-term land contracts with major urban centers across the globe. Its developments often serve as urban hubs, complete with commercial zones, entertainment venues, and high-security gated communities.

## Headquarters & Corporate Power
Ghallanda's global headquarters are located in **[[Zeal City]]’s [[Orzhov District]]**, a financial and legal powerhouse known for housing the most opulent and influential corporations. From this base of operations, Ghallanda negotiates massive land-use contracts, luxury construction projects, and expansion into untapped markets.

## Leadership: Chervina Ghallanda
The current CEO, **Chervina Ghallanda**, is the charismatic halfling granddaughter of the company’s founder, **Yoren Ghallanda**. Once a renowned celebrity and high-society icon, Chervina transitioned from the red carpet to the boardroom, bringing her media savvy and sharp business acumen to bear on the company’s continued growth. Under her leadership, Ghallanda has expanded into emerging markets and increased its presence in digital hospitality platforms and virtual tourism.

## Reputation & Influence
Ghallanda Hospitality Group is often viewed as the soft power counterbalance to the more militarized megacorps. Its influence is felt not through weapons or surveillance, but through its control of where people live, work, and sleep. Whether hosting dignitaries, housing executives, or developing new city sectors, Ghallanda ensures luxury is always part of the equation.
