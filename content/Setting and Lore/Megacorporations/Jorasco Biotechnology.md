*“Life is precious. We make it last.”*

## Overview
**Jorasco Biotechnology** (JBT) is the world’s leading provider of medical services, healthcare technology, and biomedical research. Founded by the visionary halfling **Ulara Jorasco** during the chaotic years of the **Mana Wars**, the company emerged from battlefield triage tents and arcane experiments into a global medical powerhouse. Jorasco today operates the most advanced and exclusive hospitals and clinics in the world, with its cutting-edge technologies and treatment protocols integrated into nearly every major medical center on the planet.

## Medical Services & Facilities
Jorasco’s hospitals are renowned for their combination of **high-tech science** and **magical healing**. Their premier facilities employ not only expert doctors and AI diagnostics, but also **clerics of [[Religion#Pantheon|The Physician]]**, a religious order specializing in divine healing arts. These facilities often include **on-site shrines or chapels** dedicated to The Physician, blurring the line between science and faith. Patients in these elite locations receive integrated care involving biotech, genetic enhancement, and restorative magic—services often unattainable elsewhere.

## Subscription Model
Access to Jorasco's medical services is regulated through a **tiered monthly subscription model**, making healthcare a luxury commodity in the cyberpunk era:

* **Copper (500 cp/month)** – Basic access to Jorasco facilities, diagnostics, and non-emergency care.  
* **Silver (5,000 cp/month)** – Priority scheduling, reduced wait times, and basic corporate-level care.  
* **Gold (50,000 cp/month)** – Premium care, access to advanced procedures, and eligibility for emergency dispatch via ground ambulance.  
* **Platinum (500,000 cp/month)** – Ultra-elite membership, access to luxury hospitals, and priority emergency extraction via airlift or magical teleportation.

While **Copper** is the most common individual tier, **Silver** and **Gold** are often covered by corporate employment packages. **Platinum** is reserved for the rich and powerful, and includes concierge-level care with immediate intervention from top-tier personnel.

## Rapid Response Unit (RRU)
The **Rapid Response Unit (RRU)** is Jorasco's elite emergency team, deployed during critical situations for **Gold and Platinum** subscribers. These paramedical strike teams arrive via armored ambulances or advanced air vehicles, equipped with cybernetic triage gear, magical wards, and sometimes full clerical strike squads. In extreme cases, teleportation is used to transport the patient directly to a Jorasco flagship hospital. **Copper and Silver subscribers**, by contrast, are responsible for their own transportation and are not eligible for RRU deployment.

## Research & Innovation

Beyond direct care, Jorasco is a pioneer in **genetics and meta-genetics**, having heavily invested in this area over the past two decades. Their research divisions work on gene therapies, magical resilience enhancement, regenerative medicine, and disease eradication—often exploring the blurred boundaries between natural biology and arcane alteration.

Jorasco also maintains a close relationship with **Zeal University**, funding major research departments and cutting-edge medical trials. The partnership has made Zeal U a leader in medical science and one of the primary recruitment pools for Jorasco’s research staff.

## Headquarters & Global Reach
Jorasco's headquarters are located in the **[[Orzhov District]] of [[Zeal City]]**, a hub of wealth, power, and influence. From this command center, the corporation coordinates a global network of hospitals, biotech research centers, mobile clinics, and emergency response units. With operations in nearly every major city, Jorasco is deeply embedded in the infrastructure of global health.

## Reputation & Influence
Jorasco Biotechnology is both **admired and criticized**—praised for its miraculous medical breakthroughs and life-saving interventions, but scrutinized for the monetization of care. Critics argue that tying emergency health access to income is unethical, while supporters claim the model funds innovation and ensures top-tier quality for those who can afford it.
