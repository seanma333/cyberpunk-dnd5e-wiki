*“Your wealth, our vaults—forever secure.”*

## Overview
The **Kundarak Banking Company (KBC)** is the largest and most powerful financial institution in the world. As a **multinational universal bank** and holding company, KBC oversees the majority of global capital, insurance, and asset management services. It is not only the financial backbone of nations but also the architect of the world’s most advanced **security systems**, both physical and digital.

From elite biometric vaults and quantum-encrypted data cores to full-spectrum corporate firewalls, KBC provides **unbreachable security solutions** for megacorps, world governments, and the ultra-wealthy. With assets in nearly every corner of the economy, KBC is less a company and more a **financial regime**—the hidden hand that guides the market.

## Historical Origins
KBC was originally established by the **Kundarak Clan**, a pre-industrial dwarven banking dynasty that accumulated vast fortunes long before the rise of modern capitalism. Their early mastery of metalcraft and security vault construction evolved over centuries into a global financial empire. The clan’s crest—two crossed keys over a granite vault—remains KBC’s enduring symbol of security and discretion.

## Political Influence & Board Reform
Historically, KBC wielded **unparalleled influence** over governments and corporations alike, leveraging **massive sovereign debts and inter-corporate loans** to dictate terms across borders. This dominance led to growing tensions among the other twelve megacorporations. To preserve balance, a **mutual accord** was established in which **each megacorp appoints a representative** to the KBC **Board of Directors**. While KBC remains an independent entity, this oversight prevents it from favoring any single megacorporation—at least publicly.

## Leadership & Succession Crisis
KBC is currently led by **Morrikan Irongate**, a pragmatic and highly skilled dwarf financier who is the **first non-Kundarak** bloodline CEO in the company’s centuries-old history. His appointment has triggered an ongoing **succession dispute** with members of the Kundarak Clan, who view the move as a betrayal of their legacy. This corporate-political drama continues to play out in the public eye, especially in financial circles and dwarven enclaves.

## Zeal City Operations
KBC's **headquarters in [[Zeal City]]** is located in the **[[Orzhov District]]**, known for housing the upper echelons of wealth and control. The sprawling KBC tower is both a financial nerve center and a fortress, protected by layered digital defenses, anti-teleport wards, and private security augmented with proprietary AI oversight.

## Financial Services & Security Tech
As a **universal bank**, KBC offers:

* Corporate and personal banking  
* Investment management  
* Insurance and risk mitigation  
* Corporate loans and sovereign debt servicing  
* AI-powered financial forecasting  
* Real-time encrypted asset transfers  
* Custom security solutions for both physical locations and digital infrastructure

Its security division is the **gold standard** for vault protection, server encryption, and high-value asset transport. Some of the world’s most sensitive and dangerous items—both physical and digital—are held within KBC-secured facilities.

## Reputation & Global Role
KBC is often referred to as “**The Bank of the World**.” To many, it is a symbol of order and stability in a chaotic age. To others, it represents the hidden oligarchic forces controlling global affairs from behind polished walls and armored vaults. Its neutrality is both a shield and a sword, allowing it to exist above political strife—while still pulling strings behind the scenes.
