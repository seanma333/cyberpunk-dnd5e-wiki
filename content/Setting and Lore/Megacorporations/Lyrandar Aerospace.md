*“Where the skies end, we begin.”*

## Overview
**Lyrandar Aerospace** is a leading megacorporation specializing in the design, manufacture, and deployment of **aircraft, seafaring vessels, and orbital technologies**. As one of the world’s foremost companies in **aeronautics and international logistics**, Lyrandar plays a central role in the global infrastructure of trade, travel, and communication. With its growing presence in **orbital research and space exploration**, Lyrandar is rapidly redefining itself as a company with its eyes on the stars.

## Corporate Evolution
Originally founded during the industrial age as **Lyrandar Shipping**, the company began as a family-run enterprise focused on oceanic trade routes and naval engineering. Over time, Lyrandar expanded into airship and aircraft production. In response to increasing investment in orbital infrastructure and aerospace innovation, the company formally rebranded as **Lyrandar Aerospace** three decades ago, signaling its transformation into a next-generation transport and aerospace corporation.

## Aerospace & Maritime Technology
Lyrandar manufactures a wide range of **air and sea vehicles**, from cargo freighters and luxury skycruisers to high-performance aerial drones and naval transport ships. Their proprietary **aerothrusters**, hybrid engines, and weather-adaptive hull designs are considered top-tier in the global market. They also run one of the largest **international shipping networks**, moving cargo across borders, oceans, and now, orbital space.

## Orbital Development & Skytower
Lyrandar’s greatest technological achievement to date is the **Skytower Orbital Station**, launched twenty years ago and currently in low planetary orbit. Officially designated as a **research facility**, Skytower is believed to house classified experiments in zero-gravity manufacturing, AI-enhanced atmospheric analytics, and long-range propulsion. It serves as a staging point for the company’s **emerging space exploration initiatives**, which aim to push Lyrandar beyond Ryse’s bounds in the coming decades.

## Leadership: Esravash Lyrandar
At the helm of the company is **Esravash Lyrandar**, a half-elf and direct descendant of the company’s founder. At just 30 years old, she is the **youngest CEO** among the thirteen megacorporations. Esravash is a bold, ambitious leader known for blending innovation with tradition—carrying forward the Lyrandar family legacy while aggressively pushing the company into new technological frontiers. Her leadership has earned her both admiration and scrutiny within the corporate elite.

## Zeal City Operations
Lyrandar’s major facilities in **[[Zeal City]]** are located in the **[[Izzet District]]**, an industrial and technological hub known for its experimental labs and heavy manufacturing infrastructure. The district hosts Lyrandar’s main **vehicle fabrication yards, logistics command centers**, and **orbital uplink terminals**.

## Reputation & Market Influence
Lyrandar Aerospace is seen as a vital enabler of global and intercontinental trade, as well as a pioneer of post-atmospheric infrastructure. Their blend of **engineering excellence**, **logistics mastery**, and **exploratory vision** positions them as both a practical necessity in the global economy and a symbol of humanity’s future beyond Ryse.
