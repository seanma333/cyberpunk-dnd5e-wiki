*“Order for hire. Justice by design.”*

## Overview
**Medani Security Group** is the world’s most prevalent provider of **public security and paramilitary law enforcement services**, operating under contract with nearly every local and national government on the planet. Sometimes praised and sometimes derided as the global “**rent-a-cop**” megacorp, Medani achieved its corporate ascendance not through cutting-edge tech or political manipulation, but through **sheer ubiquity**—a presence in virtually every municipality’s annual budget.

With operations ranging from **urban patrols and crowd control** to **counter-insurgency and magical enforcement**, Medani’s reach is both wide and deeply entrenched. In a world where governments are often fractured, underfunded, or heavily corporate-aligned, **outsourcing public safety to Medani** has become the default global standard.

## Public Contracts & Ubiquity
Rather than enforcing its own laws, Medani functions as a **contractual enforcement arm** of governmental bodies. These contracts—typically spanning **multiple years**—allow cities, nations, and even corporate districts to effectively privatize their law enforcement while maintaining the illusion of public control.

In **[[Zeal City]]**, this decentralized approach is taken to its extreme: each district independently negotiates its security coverage with Medani, tailoring the terms of service to fit its unique needs and governance structure. As a result, Medani maintains a **network of branch offices** across all districts, each acting as a local command center for patrols, dispatch, and citizen interaction.

## The Lawmages
Medani’s most iconic and elite division is the **Lawmages**—**gifted spellcasters** trained in arcane enforcement and legally-bound magical restraint. Unlike standard officers, Lawmages are deployed only during **high-risk or high-profile incidents**, including magical crimes, corporate terrorism, or runaway synthetic entities.

Lawmages undergo a **multi-year training regimen** at a **secretive academy**, whose location remains undisclosed even to most Medani employees. They are known for their immaculate uniforms, spell-inscribed badges, and high-profile presence at public events and crisis zones. In Zeal City, the company’s **headquarters in the [[Azorius District]]** is known as **Lawmage Plaza**, both in recognition of their prestige and as a public relations centerpiece.

## Corporate Leadership
Medani is led by **Taldor Winslow**, a half-elf and former Lawmage who rose through the company ranks after serving on several high-profile enforcement operations. Winslow is known for maintaining **strict operational protocols**, a **strong public image**, and a belief that **order must be bought before it can be maintained**. Under his leadership, Medani has expanded further into **predictive crime AI**, **magical surveillance**, and **corporate espionage countermeasures**.

## Reputation & Controversy
Public opinion of Medani is highly polarized. Some view the company as an efficient and necessary arm of modern governance—especially in cities and nations that lack the funding or cohesion to maintain public safety on their own. Others see Medani as a **tool of systemic oppression**, beholden not to justice, but to **whoever pays their contract fee**.

Critics also argue that the widespread privatization of law enforcement has created a **dangerous consolidation of power**, allowing Medani to influence legislation, suppress dissent, and enforce selectively. Nevertheless, its dominance is uncontested.
