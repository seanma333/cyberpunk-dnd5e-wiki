*“Moving the world, one line at a time.”*

## Overview
**Orien Enterprises** is a global megacorporation specializing in **land vehicle manufacturing**, **logistics and freight shipping**, **postal services**, and **consumer retail distribution**. Once known as **Orien Railways**, the company has since evolved into a full-spectrum **transportation and supply chain conglomerate**, with a presence in everything from heavy ground freight to household goods delivery.

While other megacorps focus on the skies or the stars, Orien remains dominant on the ground, maintaining one of the **most expansive and secure overland transport networks** in the world. Its signature blend of industrial might, logistical precision, and retail accessibility has made it one of the most essential—and omnipresent—corporate powers of the cyberpunk age.

## Historical Roots & Rail Dominance
Founded as **Orien Railways**, the company originally revolutionized industrial-age commerce through its **continent-spanning rail lines**, connecting distant nations with reliable freight and passenger routes. While many corporations abandoned overland travel in favor of air or sea, Orien **doubled down** on its terrestrial expertise.

To this day, the **Orien Rail Network** remains a vital artery for global shipping—especially for cargo too massive or dangerous for aerial delivery. In some parts of the world, particularly in **uninhabitable wastelands** where air routes are blocked or magical interference occurs, Orien rails are **the only viable shipping option**. These routes are fortified and patrolled to protect against **roaming monstrosities and raiders**, with Orien investing heavily in armored trains, automated defense systems, and mercenary escorts.

## Zeal City Operations
In **[[Zeal City]]**, Orien is responsible for the construction and management of the **Metro System**, a sprawling subterranean transit network that connects the city’s industrial zones, commercial hubs, and residential districts. The metro is not only vital for daily commuters but also for the movement of high-volume goods beneath the surface of one of the world's most densely populated cities.

* **Manufacturing operations** are headquartered in the **[[Izzet District]]**, where Orien constructs a wide array of land vehicles ranging from personal autos to armored convoys.

* **Logistics and transport coordination** are managed from their offices in the **[[Azorius District]]**, streamlining inner-city and long-distance shipping alike.

## Expansion into Retail & Delivery
In recent decades, Orien Enterprises has aggressively expanded into **consumer retail services**, offering both **online and offline storefronts**, as well as **last-mile delivery networks** powered by automated vehicles and courier drones. Their retail platform is a direct competitor to corporate marketplaces run by other megacorps, and they are one of the few capable of handling **both heavy industry freight and small-package delivery** under a unified system.

## Rivalry with Lyrandar
Orien's closest competitor is **[[Lyrandar Aerospace]]**, with both megacorps vying for dominance in **global shipping and logistics contracts**. While Lyrandar dominates air and sea, Orien maintains a stranglehold on **ground-based logistics and regional delivery**, making the two companies both **rivals and reluctant partners** in global trade networks.

## Leadership
Orien Enterprises is helmed by **Delilah Orien**, a human executive and the **grand-niece of the previous CEO**. Under her leadership, the company has embraced aggressive expansion into **automated freight**, **urban delivery infrastructure**, and **consumer-facing services**, without losing its industrial roots. Delilah is known for her no-nonsense leadership style and focus on resilience and adaptability—qualities that have kept Orien relevant in a rapidly evolving world.

## Reputation
Orien is widely viewed as **dependable, grounded, and essential**, though often critiqued for its monopoly on overland routes in regions with limited transport options. Despite being less flashy than other megacorps, its reliability and logistics power make it **one of the most quietly influential players** on the global stage.
