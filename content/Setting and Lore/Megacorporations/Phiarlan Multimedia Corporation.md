*“We see it first. We show it best.”*

## Overview
**Phiarlan Multimedia Corporation (PMC)** is the **largest media conglomerate in the world**, controlling a vast network of **entertainment studios, news agencies, broadcast platforms, and data distribution services**. With operations spanning nearly every continent, PMC is the primary source of both **global news coverage and cultural content**, ranging from blockbuster holovids to government-sanctioned local reports.

Founded during the **Industrial Revolution**, PMC has grown into one of the most culturally influential megacorporations, shaping public opinion, storytelling, and truth across every medium. Its unmatched ability to **acquire exclusive information**—often before events are made public—has earned it a reputation for **preternatural access to sources**, and sparked both awe and suspicion across corporate and public sectors alike.

## Media Empire & Operations
PMC owns and operates dozens of major **news networks, film studios, streaming platforms, and virtual entertainment companies**, many of which retain their own branding but are fully integrated under the Phiarlan umbrella. Its coverage ranges from **celebrity gossip to wartime frontline reports**, and it often forms **official partnerships with local and national governments** to provide controlled media for the public.

Despite this close involvement with political powers, PMC’s journalism division is widely respected for its **speed, accuracy, and depth of access**, often delivering breaking news well before its rivals.

Phiarlan is frequently scrutinized for potential **bias or manipulation**, especially in authoritarian regimes where their government contracts have blurred the lines between information and propaganda. Nonetheless, it remains the most **watched, downloaded, and quoted** media entity on the planet.

## Zeal City Divisions
In **[[Zeal City]]**, PMC maintains a dual presence, reflecting the split between its entertainment and journalism operations:

* The **Entertainment Division** is based in the **[[Rakdos District]]**, where it produces immersive dramas, high-budget VR experiences, and celebrity-driven content for global audiences.  
* The **News Division** is headquartered in the **[[Dimir District]]**, a shadowy area known for surveillance tech and information control—fitting for a company that thrives on secrets and scoops.

## Leadership & Legacy
The company is led by its founder and eternal figurehead, **Elvinor Elorenthi Phiarlan**, a high elf **over 300 years old**, who has personally overseen the company’s evolution from print media to global infotainment powerhouse. Her longevity and sharp intellect have made her one of the most respected—and feared—figures in corporate circles.

Thirty years ago, Elvinor had a **very public and highly contentious falling out** with her son, **Elar Thuranni Phiarlan**, who disagreed with her approach to editorial independence and corporate governance. After being **publicly disowned**, Elar went on to found his own rival media empire, **[[Thuranni Media Technologies]]**, which has risen rapidly and now directly challenges Phiarlan for global influence.

Their feud is often portrayed in tabloids as a corporate family drama—but to insiders, it represents **two competing visions of media power**, and a looming **information cold war** between mother and son.

## Reputation
Phiarlan is seen by many as the **gold standard of media influence**—sophisticated, omnipresent, and always in-the-know. Others view it as a manipulative giant with too much control over what people see, hear, and believe. Regardless of public perception, **no one in the media world moves without considering Phiarlan’s shadow**.
