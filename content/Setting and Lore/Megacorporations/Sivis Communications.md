 *“We connect the world—and beyond.”*

## Overview
**Sivis Communications**, commonly known as **Sivcom**, is the **world’s first and largest telecommunications and internet service provider**, and the **backbone of the global digital network**. From **high-speed infrastructure** and **mobile data services** to **web hosting and enterprise IT solutions**, Sivcom is synonymous with connectivity in the cyberpunk age. Nearly **every digital interaction on the planet**—from casual street browsing to top-tier corporate transactions—passes through Sivcom’s systems.

Despite its scale and reach, the company is infamous for its **tiered service structure**, where **connection quality is directly tied to subscription level**. While the rich enjoy flawless, unthrottled access, **low-tier users often experience latency, random throttling, and restricted access**, leading to frequent complaints but few alternatives—**Sivcom is simply too integral to avoid.**

## Services & Infrastructure
Sivcom provides:

* **Telecom services** (mobile, satellite, and hardline)  
* **Internet access**, with coverage extending even into remote and hazardous zones  
* **Data centers and cloud hosting**  
* **Enterprise-level cybersecurity and AI analytics**  
* **Intra- and intercity communications networks**

In recent years, Sivcom has pivoted toward **web-based IT solutions**, becoming a **dominant force in cloud computing, virtual identity hosting, and augmented neural net services**. Many megacorporations run their internal systems on Sivcom platforms, creating a vast interdependence between Sivcom and the rest of the corporate world.

## Interplanar Ambitions
CEO and founder **Alston Warrick Sivis**, a famously **eccentric gnome technomancer**, has been at the company’s helm since its inception. Known for his bizarre yet brilliant ideas, Sivis is currently pursuing a **revolutionary project**: the creation of a **large-scale interplanar communication system**, blending **arcane portals with quantum-entangled networks**. Though mocked in public and feared in private, Sivis’s vision continues to draw massive research funding—and quiet interest from powerful factions.

## Zeal City Presence
Sivcom’s headquarters, **Sivis Tower**, is located in the **heart of the [[Dimir District]]**, [[Zeal City]]'s **tech and information nerve center**. The tower is surrounded by high-security data vaults, AI research firms, and competitive start-ups, making it a **fortress of digital power**. Inside, the company runs its **Global Operations Nexus**, where data from around the world—and soon, perhaps, other planes—is routed and managed.

## Subscription Model & Controversy
Sivcom’s **tiered subscription system** is legendary—and divisive. Users are sorted into categories based on how much they pay:

* **Platinum & Corporate Tiers** – Ultra-fast, unrestricted access with priority routing  
* **Gold** – High-speed, business-class services  
* **Silver** – Moderate speed, with occasional throttling  
* **Bronze & Street Access** – Minimal service, heavy throttling, intrusive ads

This stratification has **widened the digital divide** in many urban zones, making full internet access another measure of corporate hierarchy and privilege. Activists and hacker collectives frequently protest Sivcom's practices, but with **no serious competitor** offering comparable global coverage, these complaints rarely move the needle.

## Reputation
Sivcom is both **ubiquitous and unavoidable**—an omnipresent force in modern life. Users rely on it, resent it, and **cannot function without it**. Some view the company as a **necessary evil**, others as a **technological marvel**, and a few as **a dangerous monopoly with ambitions beyond reality**.
