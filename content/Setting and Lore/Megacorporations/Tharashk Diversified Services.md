*“Beyond the edge of civilization, we hold the line.”*

## Overview
**Tharashk Diversified Services (TDS)** is one of the **most unconventional megacorporations** in the world, specializing in **paramilitary operations and expeditionary logistics** in hazardous, undeveloped, and post-apocalyptic zones. Unlike other security-focused corporations, Tharashk's operations take place almost entirely **outside city limits**, where civilization fades into **ruin, wilderness, or wasteland**.

Formed in the aftermath of the **Mana Wars**, TDS quickly established itself as a vital player in **supply chain protection, area reclamation, and artifact recovery**, and has grown into the go-to corporation for high-risk ventures into the untamed world.

## Operations & Services
Tharashk provides elite **combat-survival squads** trained not for police action or urban conflict, but for long-term deployment in wild, dangerous zones. Their services include:

* **Convoy and shipment protection** across undeveloped regions  
* **Rail line security**, especially for **[[Orien Enterprises]]**, with whom they have a long-standing and exclusive protection contract  
* **Monstrosity hunting and extermination** in areas targeted for development  
* **Magical artifact recovery**, especially from ruins left behind after the Mana Wars

Many TDS squads operate autonomously for weeks or months at a time, engaging not only with dangerous wildlife and magical anomalies, but also with **nomadic raiders, warbands, and unstable mana zones**.

## Artifact Recovery Division
One of the most lucrative divisions of Tharashk involves **scavenging ruins of the pre-Mana War civilizations** for powerful **magical artifacts and relics**. These are either:

* Sold through discreet luxury auctions to **wealthy collectors and arcane enthusiasts**, or  
* Broken down and refined into **pure residuum**, a valuable substance used in enchantment and power generation

This division often draws criticism from historians and conservationists, who accuse Tharashk of **looting magical heritage** for profit—but the company insists its work contributes to civilization’s advancement and survival.

## Weapons Manufacturing
Though better known for its field operations, TDS is also the **second-largest weapons manufacturer in the world**, trailing only **[[Deneith Armaments Corporation]]**. Tharashk weapons are favored for their **rugged durability and modular design**, tailored for use in harsh and unpredictable environments.

## Leadership
TDS is led by **Magrim Torm**, a **half-orc veteran strategist** and the daughter of a celebrated general from the **New Alliance** during the Mana Wars. Magrim’s military background has deeply influenced the company’s culture—practical, results-driven, and ruthlessly efficient. Under her leadership, TDS has expanded its reach into **extraterritorial operations**, often negotiating directly with city-states and megacorps to secure reclamation contracts and artifact recovery rights.

## Zeal City Presence
Tharashk’s main offices and training facilities in **[[Zeal City]]** are located in the **[[Boros District]]**, which houses many of the city’s military and industrial operations. While its urban presence is minimal compared to other megacorps, Tharashk maintains **a constant flow of personnel and supplies** through its city hub, which also serves as a debriefing point for returning expeditions.

## Reputation
Tharashk is known as the **megacorp that goes where others refuse**—a reputation that has earned them both respect and wariness. They are **essential to global logistics and expansion**, particularly as humanity pushes further into **dangerous ruins and reclamation zones**. At the same time, their **mercenary mindset** and willingness to **operate beyond traditional legal frameworks** make them controversial players in geopolitical circles.
