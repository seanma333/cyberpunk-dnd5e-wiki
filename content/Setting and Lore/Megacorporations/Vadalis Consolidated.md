*“We Feed the Future.”*

## Overview
 Vadalis Consolidated is the world’s largest food production megacorporation, responsible for supplying a majority of the synthetic and processed food consumed across the globe. Though originally a traditional agricultural company, Vadalis has grown into a corporate powerhouse by adapting to post-war environmental collapse through the mass production of artificial nutrition, factory-farmed meat, and subterranean agriculture. As one of the 13 megacorporations, Vadalis plays a critical role in maintaining the planetary food supply—sometimes controversially.

## Operations
Vadalis specializes in a wide range of food technologies, from high-efficiency synthetic meal packs for urban populations to gene-tailored livestock bred on offshore island facilities. While synthetic food is its largest market, the company has invested heavily in both reclaiming usable land and advancing subterranean farming technologies, particularly in and beneath [[Zeal City]]. The company’s holdings include multiple fertile islands—many of which are rumored to have been seized from native populations by hired paramilitary forces.

The company’s headquarters is located in **Vadalis Arcology**, a vast and self-sustaining arcology in the **[[Golgari District]]** of **Zeal City**. The structure functions as a vertical city, complete with living quarters for employees, internal food production labs, groundwater reserves, research facilities, and independent power systems. Beneath the arcology lies an extensive tunnel system containing underground greenhouses and vertical farms—some of the only such operations functioning at scale in the post-war era.

## Divisions and Services

* **SynFoods** – Mass-produced synthetic meals and nutrition packs.  
* **Vadalis Agriculture** – Traditional and experimental agricultural development, often on controlled island environments.  
* **DeepGreen** – Subterranean and hydroponic farming systems under urban centers.  
* **Beastline Genetics** – Animal husbandry and engineered livestock breeding.

## Leadership
The company is currently led by **Darren Calder**, a human executive who gained control of the company through marriage to **Mazina Vadalis**, daughter of former CEO **Gazir Vadalis**. Calder’s leadership has not gone uncontested—**Sazik Vadalis**, Mazina’s brother, has publicly denounced his brother-in-law’s corporate strategy, leading to a well-known internal family rivalry that plays out in the media and boardroom alike.

## Controversy
Despite its role in feeding much of the world’s population, Vadalis Consolidated is the subject of numerous ethical and legal disputes. Critics point to the company’s alleged use of force in acquiring its island territories, the environmental damage caused by factory farming, and its monopolistic control over food distribution in many regions. Still, its grip on the global food chain has proven unshakable, particularly in post-war urban environments where traditional farming is no longer viable.

## Presence in Zeal City
Vadalis’s operations in Zeal City are centered in the **Golgari District**, where the **Vadalis Arcology** serves as both headquarters and operational hub. The arcology’s subterranean levels contain some of the largest functioning underground farms on the continent, making it a key part of the city’s food infrastructure.
