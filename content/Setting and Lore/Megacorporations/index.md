aliases:

There are thousands of companies active in the world, but only a select few have risen to claim the title of **megacorporation**: worldwide conglomerates whose power and influence is so grand they act almost as sovereign nations themselves. In [[Zeal City]], 13 megacorporations have the greatest political power, with each one having a representative in the city's Executive Council. The security forces of each megacorporation are equivalent in strength to a small nation's military, and each one heavily spends resources on R&D for both scientific and magical purposes.

Each megacorporation has multiple horizontal divisions spanning different industries to compete against each other, but the industries listed are where each one specializes and excels.
