## History

The Black Lotus Triad traces its origins to the **Eastern Dragon Empires** — ancient magetocracies where bloodlines tied to the great dragons determined who ruled, who studied magic, and who survived. These empires were among the most powerful in the old world, and they fell hard. The [[Setting and Lore/index#Welcome to the New Age|Mana Wars]] shattered the imperial order, and the diaspora that followed brought migrants from the Eastern lands to cities across the new world, including [[Zeal City]].

In Zeal City, Eastern immigrants settled in what would become **Lantern Town**, and the Black Lotus Triad came with them — not as conquerors, but as protectors. In a city with little patience for unfamiliar communities, the Triad offered what the law would not: safety, dispute resolution, and a community that looked after its own. Over time, that protection became an empire. But Lantern Town remains the heart.

## Structure

The Black Lotus Triad follows a strict hierarchical structure rooted in the traditions of the Eastern Empires, with each rank carrying specific titles and responsibilities.

- **Dragon's Head (489)** — Supreme leader. Identity unknown to all but the innermost circle. Communicates through intermediaries. May not be based in Zeal City.
- **Mountain Master (438)** — *"Auntie."* The operational leader and public face of the Triad in Zeal City. All day-to-day decisions flow through her.
- **Incense Master (438)** — Internal enforcer. Handles discipline and matters the Mountain Master cannot be seen to touch.
- **Red Pole (426)** — Military commander. Oversees the Triad's muscle and coordinates enforcement operations.
- **White Paper Fan (415)** — Administrative and financial officer. Manages the books, bribes, and legitimate business fronts.
- **Straw Sandal (432)** — Liaison and messenger. Maintains external relationships, including with fixers and Blue Lanterns.
- **Blue Lanterns** — Uninitiated associates. Skilled operators — lancers, fixers, specialists — who work in the Triad's interests without formal membership. Protected while useful. Cut loose without ceremony when they are not.

## Territory: Lantern Town

The Black Lotus Triad's home is **Lantern Town**, a dense neighborhood in the western quarter of [[Rakdos District|Rakdos]] that functions as the cultural heart of Zeal City's Eastern immigrant community. Paper lanterns line the streets — red and gold by day, every color at night. The signs are in half a dozen Eastern scripts. The food is real, not synthetic. The temples are old.

The Triad does not advertise its presence in Lantern Town. It doesn't need to. The community knows who keeps the streets safe, who handles disputes that [[Medani Security Group|Medani]] won't touch, and who to call when something goes wrong.

The Triad's most prominent venue is **The Bunker** — a nightclub converted from a war-era shelter near Lantern Town's main thoroughfare. The thick walls and original construction block signals in and out; everything inside runs on a wired internal server. No recordings, no transmissions, no surveillance. For a criminal organization conducting business in public, the isolation is not a quirk. It's the point. The Bunker is where deals get made.

## Rackets

- **Protection** — The foundation of their operations in Lantern Town, where the Triad functions as an unofficial government for the community: dispute resolution, security, and social services, bundled into one arrangement with no opt-out.
- **Smuggling** — The Eastern Dragon Empires produced magical traditions and alchemical knowledge that survived the wars in fragments. The Triad specializes in Eastern magical goods — components, formulas, ritual items — that have no equivalent source in Zeal City. If it came from the other side of the world and it isn't on any manifest, it probably moved through Lantern Town.
- **The Fixer Network** — Auntie maintains an extensive web of lancers and independent operators who handle work the Triad needs done without direct involvement. It is one of the most comprehensive lancer networks in the city.
- **Gambling and Entertainment** — Several Lantern Town venues run games blending Eastern and Western traditions, with The Bunker serving as both entertainment hub and operational front.

## Reputation

The Black Lotus Triad is regarded with a mixture of respect and wariness by the other Houses. They are not the oldest, but they are arguably the most disciplined — the hierarchical structure enforces accountability in ways looser organizations cannot match. They do not make commitments lightly, and they do not forget debts in either direction.

Among lancers and independent operators, the Triad's reputation rests on a single principle: *Auntie's word is good, and Auntie's blacklist is permanent*. Working for the Black Lotus is steady, reasonably safe, and well-compensated. Crossing them is the last professional mistake most people make.

The Dragon's Head remains a subject of speculation. Some believe the position is ceremonial — a symbol of continuity with the old empire traditions. Others believe the Dragon's Head is very real, very powerful, and watching everything from a distance. No one who claims to know the truth has proven it.
