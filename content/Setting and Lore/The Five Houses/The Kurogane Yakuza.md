> [!info] One of the [[Setting and Lore/The Five Houses/index|Five Houses of Rakdos]]
> **Territory:** The Gardens & the Koi Hotel ([[Orzhov District|Orzhov]])

## History

The Kurogane trace their origins to the **Seiryuu Shogunate** — the Azure Dragon Empire, a coastal island nation in the Eastern Dragon Empires defined by rigid feudal hierarchy, martial tradition, and a culture that had remained deliberately insular for centuries. The Shogunate was not conquered during the [[Setting and Lore/index#Welcome to the New Age|Mana Wars]] so much as slowly strangled: trade routes severed, magical traditions suppressed, the old noble houses stripped of power by industrial interests that had no patience for feudal governance.

The diaspora that followed was not chaotic — the Seiryuu people left in organized waves, bringing their hierarchies intact. The warrior-class families that had served the Shogunate found that their skills translated well into the criminal economy of the new world. In [[Zeal City]], these families coalesced under the banner of **House Konda**, the most prominent of the Seiryuu noble lines to make the crossing, and the Kurogane was formalized as the organization through which they would maintain their power in exile.

Unlike the other Houses, the Kurogane has never pretended to be anything other than what it is: a disciplined, hierarchical organization run by a family that considers itself the rightful inheritor of Seiryuu tradition. Whether that tradition is noble or criminal is, to them, a distinction without a difference.

## Leadership: House Konda

The Kurogane is led by **Takeshi Konda**, the current patriarch and the organization's *oyabun*. In his late sixties, silver-haired and formally dressed, Takeshi carries the weight of the family name like armor — visibly, deliberately, as a statement of identity. He speaks rarely and precisely. His word is final.

Beside him in every sense that matters is **Fumiko Konda** — his wife, formally the *ane-san* of the organization, and informally the person the Kurogane's leadership is slightly more afraid of than the patriarch himself. She was not born into House Konda; she married into it, then spent thirty years becoming indispensable to it. Where Takeshi sets policy, Fumiko enforces it. The distinction between her authority and her husband's matters until it doesn't.

The family's public face — and its bridge to the legitimate world — is **Ryoji Konda**, the eldest son and the organization's *wakagashira*. On paper, Ryoji is a hospitality executive: general manager of the Koi Hotel, the Konda family's flagship property in the Orzhov District, which operates as a subsidiary of **[[Ghallanda Hospitality Group]]**. In practice, he is his father's second-in-command, managing the organization's most important strategic asset and its most sensitive corporate relationships. He is the door through which most dealings with the Kurogane begin.

## Structure

The Kurogane follows traditional Yakuza hierarchy, adapted from Seiryuu warrior-class tradition. Membership is strictly human — this is policy, enforced consistently and without public explanation.

- **Oyabun** — The patriarch. Absolute authority. Currently Takeshi Konda.
- **Wakagashira** — Second-in-command, managing day-to-day operations. Currently Ryoji Konda.
- **Shateigashira** — Regional lieutenants overseeing specific territories or operations.
- **Shatei** — "Younger brothers." Full members, bound by oath of loyalty to the family.
- **Kobun** — "Foster children." Associates and junior members still proving their worth.

## Territory: The Gardens & The Koi Hotel

The Kurogane's home territory in [[Rakdos District|Rakdos]] is **The Gardens** — a deliberate, curated neighborhood that stands apart from the surrounding district. Where Rakdos is loud and relentless, The Gardens is composed: manicured green spaces between buildings, stone lanterns lining the streets, architecture that references Seiryuu aesthetics without replicating them exactly. It is the most visually distinctive neighborhood in Rakdos, and unmistakably theirs.

Beyond The Gardens, the Kurogane holds the most geographically distributed territory of any of the Five Houses — venues, front businesses, and operational nodes spread across the wider Rakdos district. No single street defines them. They are simply present, in many places at once.

Their most significant holding outside Rakdos is the **Koi Hotel** in the Orzhov District. Operated as a Ghallanda subsidiary on paper, the Koi functions as neutral ground for all five Houses to conduct business. Disputes that would cause bloodshed anywhere else are settled in its private rooms. That this neutral ground is owned by the Kurogane is a source of leverage so refined it barely needs to be acknowledged.

## Rackets

- **Protection and Extortion** — The Kurogane's territorial reach across Rakdos makes their protection network the most extensive of the Five Houses. Businesses in their territory pay. Those that don't are reminded, once, politely.
- **Hospitality and Entertainment** — Deep roots in the industry, from the legitimate luxury of the Koi Hotel to less legitimate venues throughout Rakdos. They understand how to make people comfortable, and how to use that comfort.
- **Weapons and Martial Trade** — A holdover from Seiryuu warrior tradition. The Kurogane maintains connections to precision arms dealers and specializes in high-quality blades, custom pieces, and weapons with provenance.
- **Influence and Blackmail** — The neutral ground of the Koi Hotel means the Kurogane has witnessed more sensitive conversations than any organization in the city. They do not advertise this. They don't need to.

## Reputation

The Kurogane is regarded as the most *formal* of the Five Houses — interactions follow protocols that other Houses find either reassuring or exhausting. They do not do casual. Every meeting has a structure. Every favor has an acknowledged weight.

The Koi Hotel has made them simultaneously the most neutral and most informed faction in Rakdos. Everyone needs the neutral ground. Everyone knows the price of that neutrality is that House Konda sees everything.

Takeshi is respected as a patriarch in the traditional sense — distant, formal, the embodiment of the family's legitimacy. Fumiko is respected differently. The stories about her are not told loudly, but they are told. Ryoji is the one most people actually deal with: polished, bilingual between the criminal and corporate worlds, and more than he appears.
