> **Territory:** Sapphire Row

## History

Before the [[Setting and Lore/index#Welcome to the New Age|Mana Wars]], **Lapis Lazuli** was a merchant of rare magical components, luxury goods, and information — operating out of the elven territories, where his gift for reading people had made him quietly prosperous. When those territories burned, Lapis survived by leveraging decades of relationships built across factional lines. While the elven homelands collapsed, he had friends in the right places and the pragmatism to call in every favor he was owed.

He came to [[Zeal City]] in the early years of its post-war growth, when [[Rakdos District|Rakdos]] was still finding its identity. He saw what it would become before anyone else did. By the time the other houses arrived, Lapis was already there — and already essential. Over the following century, the Lazuli Mafia became the oldest and most established of the Five Houses, built not on violence alone but on patience, institutional memory, and the simple fact that the Don has outlived every rival who underestimated him.

## Structure

The Lazuli Mafia follows a traditional hierarchical structure with one distinctive feature: the Don's **capos are all his own children**. Over four and a half centuries, Lapis has had many children — not all of them have been invited into the family business. He is extraordinarily selective, and the criteria are known only to him. Children who don't make the cut hold no formal standing, but are not cast out. This ambiguity is deliberate.

The result is a family in perpetual, carefully managed tension. Capos are bound by blood to the Don but compete with each other in everything else — territory, resources, approval. Open conflict between siblings is forbidden. Quiet maneuvering is constant.

- **Don** — *Lapis Lazuli*. Final authority on all matters. Rarely seen in public; his presence is felt in every Lazuli operation.
- **Capos (Caporegimes)** — The Don's children. Each oversees a defined territory or operational area. The current number of active capos is not publicized.
- **Soldiers** — Made members, bound by oath. The oath has teeth; the nature of those teeth is a matter of rumor.
- **Associates** — Non-members who work with the family regularly. A reliable path to becoming a Soldier, for those who want it.

## Territory: Sapphire Row

The Lazuli family's home territory is **Sapphire Row** — a single, immaculately maintained street that cuts through Rakdos like a relic from another era. Where the Strip runs on neon and noise, Sapphire Row is quiet, warm-lit, and old. The clubs here have no signs outside. You either know what they are or you don't belong.

At its center sits **Casa Lazuli**, the family's flagship establishment — the most prestigious casino in Rakdos. Deep blue awnings, a doorman in a suit, a small lapis stone inlaid into the pavement at the entrance. The public floor is open to anyone with enough coin. The upper floors operate by invitation only. High-stakes Dragon Chess tournaments are a particular tradition, occasionally attended by the Don himself.

## Rackets

- **Protection** — Every significant venue on the Strip pays the Lazuli family. In exchange, problems are resolved quietly and outsiders are discouraged from causing trouble. Most venue owners consider it a reasonable cost of doing business.
- **Gambling** — Casa Lazuli is their most visible enterprise, blending old-world games (Dragon Chess, bone dice sets with genuine pre-war provenance) with the city's appetite for high stakes.
- **The Antiquities Trade** — Lapis's pre-war connections give the family access to magical components, pre-industrial artifacts, and legacy items that cannot be sourced through legitimate channels. They are not the only black market in Rakdos, but they are the only one with genuine provenance.
- **Information** — The Don has watched this city for over a century. His capos for decades. The family knows where the bodies are buried — sometimes literally.

## Reputation

The Lazuli family is widely regarded as the most *civilized* of the Five Houses, which is not the same as the most trustworthy.

They avoid operations that generate bad press — no trafficking, no indiscriminate violence, nothing that brings serious [[Medani Security Group|Medani]] attention to Rakdos as a whole. This restraint is partly principle and partly pragmatism; the Don has survived long enough to understand that conspicuousness is a liability.

Among civilians, they occupy that particular moral space old criminal institutions tend to settle into: vaguely glamorous, faintly threatening, occasionally useful. The blue-haired Lazuli soldiers are a fixture of certain Rakdos streets. Most people know better than to cause them problems — and know that if something needs handling, there is a door on Sapphire Row you can knock on.

**Don Lapis himself** is seen by those who've met him as someone operating on a different timescale than everyone else in the room. He is not in a hurry. He has never been in a hurry. This, more than anything, is what makes him dangerous.
