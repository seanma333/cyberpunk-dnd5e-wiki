> [!info] One of the [[Setting and Lore/The Five Houses/index|Five Houses of Rakdos]]
> **Territory:** The Briarpatch
> *Smallest house, biggest surprise.*

## History

When the [[Demographics#Fey Races|Fey Crossings]] opened fifty years ago, the city was not prepared for the arrival of an entirely new category of people. Anti-fey sentiment ran high, particularly in districts where newcomers competed for housing and work. Most established communities kept their distance. The gnomes didn't.

Gnomes had long occupied a peculiar social position in [[Zeal City]] — present in every district, employed in every industry, and consistently underestimated by everyone. Their natural affinity for the Fey and their outsider's understanding of what it meant to be overlooked made them the community most willing to extend a hand. In the northern edge of [[Rakdos District|Rakdos]], gnome residents and Fey newcomers began sharing neighborhoods, businesses, and — inevitably — criminal enterprises.

The organization that emerged became the Midsummer Crew: the smallest of the Five Houses, built not on ancient tradition or family dynasty but on the principle that the people nobody takes seriously should stick together. They have been underestimated at every stage of their existence. They have survived every single person who underestimated them.

## Leadership: Puck Goodfellow

**Puck Goodfellow** has run the Midsummer Crew for as long as anyone in the Briarpatch can remember. A gnome of indeterminate middle age with an expression of permanent mild amusement, Puck is the kind of person who makes you feel like you're in on the joke right up until you realize you're the joke.

He is the most publicly visible leader of the Five Houses — found in the Briarpatch's bars and markets most days, apparently available to anyone who wants to talk, genuinely friendly with half the neighborhood. This openness is not carelessness. Nothing about Puck Goodfellow is careless. He understands that the best place to hide is in plain sight, and that the most dangerous thing in any room is the one that doesn't look dangerous.

No one is entirely sure what Puck's actual capabilities are. The stories are varied and contradictory, which is probably intentional.

## Structure

The Midsummer Crew operates with deliberately less formal hierarchy than the other Houses — which other Houses sometimes mistake for disorganization. It isn't.

- **The Boss** — Puck Goodfellow. Sole authority. Approachable, friendly, absolute.
- **The Crew** — Four to six trusted inner circle members, no formal titles. Each runs a specific operation or territory pocket. Known to each other and to Puck; not publicly identified.
- **Associates** — A wide, loose network of gnomes, fey races, half-fey, and anyone operating under Midsummer protection. Not formal members — just people who are, as they say in the Briarpatch, *with us*.
- **Runners** — Street-level operators, often young, often fey. Fast, hard to track, intimately familiar with the Briarpatch's geography.

## Territory: The Briarpatch

The Briarpatch sits at the northern edge of Rakdos where the district bleeds toward [[Gruul District|Gruul]] — cheap real estate, industrial blight, the kind of neighborhood that formed because nowhere else would have them. It should, by all rights, be a miserable place to live.

It isn't. The Fey presence has done something to the neighborhood that no one has fully explained. Plants grow where they shouldn't. The air is cleaner than surrounding blocks. Street murals appear overnight in styles no one recognizes. The lighting has a quality at dusk that artists from across the city come to paint. It is stubbornly, inexplicably *alive* in a part of the city that has no business being alive.

The main social hub is **The Green Man** — a pub that looks like it was transplanted from another world entirely: dark wood, warm light, ivy growing up the exterior walls. It is where the Crew conducts business, where the neighborhood gathers, and where Puck Goodfellow can usually be found nursing a drink and watching the door.

## Rackets

- **Fey Substances** — The Crew's most distinctive operation. Glamour-touched compounds, dream-inducing Fey extracts, and substances with no mundane equivalent. The other Houses dabble in drugs; none has access to what the Crew can provide.
- **Smuggling** — Goods moving between the Fey community and the wider city, through channels that occasionally connect back toward the [[Selesnya District|Selesnya]] Fey Crossing. The Crew knows paths through the city that don't appear on any [[Orien Enterprises|Orien]] map.
- **Mischief for Hire** — The Crew's runners are available for jobs requiring speed, stealth, and someone no one will notice. They are very good at this.
- **Community Protection** — The Crew functions as de facto security for the Briarpatch and Zeal City's wider Fey community. Anti-fey violence in their territory is addressed promptly and without involving [[Medani Security Group|Medani]].

## Reputation

The Midsummer Crew is the House that the other four consistently underestimate, and consistently regret underestimating. Smallest organization, least prestigious territory, least formal structure — and yet they have never lost a territorial dispute they chose to fight, and have access to products and channels no other House can replicate.

Among the other Houses, the Crew is tolerated with mild condescension and genuine wariness. Nobody wants to be the organization that started a war with a gang of gnomes and fey. The optics are terrible and — more importantly — the Crew has a gift for making their enemies look foolish before making them disappear.

In the Briarpatch, the Midsummer Crew is simply home. Puck Goodfellow is the neighbor who knows everyone's name, the first call when something goes wrong, and the last person you'd want to cross. The neighborhood loves him. The feeling is, in its way, genuine.
