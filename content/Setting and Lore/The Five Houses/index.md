The Five Houses control the criminal underworld of the [[Rakdos District]]. Each operates independently but maintains an uneasy coexistence: open war between Houses is bad for business.

| House | Territory | Leader | In Brief |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| [[The Lazuli Mafia]] | Sapphire Row | Don Lapis Lazuli | Old elven money. |
| [[The Black Lotus Triad]] | Lantern Town | "Auntie," the Mountain Master | Eastern tradition. |
| [[The Rimewind Bratva]] | The Coldworks | Volk, the Pakhan | Thieves-in-law. |
| [[The Kurogane Yakuza]] | The Gardens & the Koi Hotel | Takeshi Konda, the Oyabun | Honor and leverage. |
| [[The Midsummer Crew]] | The Briarpatch | Puck Goodfellow | The ones nobody takes seriously. |

When the Houses need to settle a dispute without bloodshed, they meet on neutral ground at the Kurogane's **Koi Hotel** in the [[Orzhov District]].

See also [[Crime]] for the wider criminal underworld of [[Zeal City]].
