The Five Houses control the criminal underworld of the [[Rakdos District]]. Each operates independently but maintains an uneasy coexistence: open war between Houses is bad for business.

When the Houses need to settle a dispute without bloodshed, they meet on neutral ground at the Kurogane's **Koi Hotel** in the [[Orzhov District]].

## The Street War

The current peace between the Houses is younger than it looks. Several years ago, a dispute between the Houses boiled over into an all-out gang war fought in the streets of Rakdos. For months, no venue was safe, shootouts spilled onto the Strip, and every House lost people it couldn't afford to lose.

The war ended not because anyone won, but because everyone was bleeding. The Houses agreed to a truce, and the Koi Hotel became the place where they would settle their differences from then on. The peace has held since, but it's an uneasy one: old grudges from the war haven't been forgotten, and every House is still counting its dead.
