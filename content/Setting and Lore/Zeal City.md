The primary setting is **Zeal City**, a sprawling modern metropolis with a population of 30 million. The city is one of a few places in the world which was left fully intact after the wars, and its size and influence has grown considerably since then. It now acts as an independent city-state and is the largest city in Ryse.

## History

Centuries ago, before the Industrial Revolution, the **Zeal Peninsula** was the domain of a greedy and powerful dragon named **Xanathos**. Over the course of a millennium, Xanathos had collected an enormous treasure hoard in the center of the peninsula. The clever Xanathos made sure this was known to the world, as the peninsula became one of the most desirable areas for thrill-seeking adventurers to try to conquer, only for them to fall to the mighty dragon and his minions. And with their defeat, his hoard of magical treasures grew enormous.

It was only when a group of 10 powerful adventurers banded together where Xanathos and his minions finally fell. The adventurers then established their own territories on the peninsula, with the ten becoming known as the **Founders**.

As the Industrial Revolution began, the massive hoard of Xanathos' treasures was melted down to a significant amount of Residuum, establishing the peninsula as a central place to develop technology. It was further discovered that the Peninsula had a large reserve of Residuum buried in its northern mountain range, perfect for mining. People began migrating to the territories in the Peninsula in droves seeking new opportunities, and the individual territories grew in size and population. When the Mana Wars escalated to a climax, the ten territories joined together to form what is now Zeal City, with each of the individual territories becoming municipalities of the united city-state. It was this union that allowed the city to survive the wars almost completely unharmed.

Over time, people from all over the world moved to Zeal City, causing a population boom. Corporations producing the technology that won the war found that the city had an almost unlimited supply of labor. It wasn't long before the city became run by the rich and powerful, while most can only hope for a content but mundane life.

## Life in the City

The majority of people in Zeal City live and work for one of the thousands of companies in the city, making enough for a modest lifestyle in a small apartment and keeping them fed and off the streets. For those that were unable to keep up a job or have low income, the streets are the only choice for a home. Upper class districts are strict with policing and kicking out vagrants, while the streets of the lower-class districts are overrun with gangs and organized crime. It is very common to see people carrying weapons around with them as they walk through the dangerous streets and alleyways of the city, even if they are not criminals.

The rich and powerful live high above the streets in their skyscraper towers. In the upper class areas, sky bridges connect many of the skyscrapers to each other so that the rich do not need to walk the dangerous streets, and if they must, they normally have heavily armed bodyguards to protect them.

In many areas of the city, crime runs rampant on the streets. Anarchist gangs parade around, causing property damage and claiming turf for their own against other gangs. Organized Crime has become incredibly profitable, selling black market goods and extorting protection money from people who cannot count on the law to protect them. 

## Geography

![[Zeal City District Map.png]]

Zeal City consists of the Peninsula and a few islands.The northern border of the city is protected by a mountain range, and separates the city from a vast wasteland beyond filled with monstrosities. The city is approximately 2500 square miles (6500 square kilometers) in total land area.
