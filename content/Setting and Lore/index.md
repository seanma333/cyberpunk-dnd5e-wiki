## Welcome to the New Age

The setting takes place on **Ryse**, an Earth-like planet in the Material Plane. Magic exists on Ryse, but is only naturally accessible to individuals who are able to channel **Mana**, a type of energy from which all Magic is derived. Such individuals were known as the **Gifted**, comprising about 1% of the total population. You are more likely to be born with the gift if either biological parent is gifted, but even then it is rare, about a 5% chance if both parents are gifted. For most of history, the gifted held the political and social power in the world.

However, everything changed with the development of **Residuum Mining**. Residuum is a rare mineral that was used in the creation of magical items, and in its purest form, can be burned to generate Mana. Its rarity meant that it was not generally usable for the general populace, until it was discovered that natural residuum reserves existed all over Ryse, and it could be extracted in mass quantities. This discovery sparked an **Industrial Revolution**: rapid technological progress with residuum as an energy source (similar to coal or petroleum on Earth) gave benefits to the common people which only the gifted had seen before. New nations were being formed as people began leaving the often tyrannical rule of the gifted ones to be part of this new world.

It wasn't long before those formerly in power saw their influence slipping away. 30 years after the Industrial Revolution began, the old nations formed an alliance (calling themselves the **Coalition**) and declared war on the new nations. In response, the new nations formed their own military alliance (calling themselves the **New World**) and pooled their knowledge and resources to develop new military technology, such as advanced firearms and fast communication networks.

This led to the **Mana Wars**, a decades-long series of escalating conflicts where magic and technology clashed. After almost 20 years of war, the New World won against the Coalition, but it was a pyrrhic victory.  Most nations in the world were severely damaged or outright annihilated, and the fallout from the wars left 75% of arable land in the world as dangerous wastelands populated by monstrosities. The surviving population began migrating to the few remaining livable areas, and the resulting population boom resulted in mass urbanization and growth in those territories.

It has been 150 years since the wars ended, and the remaining nations have rebuilt themselves. However, the world is different. Powerful corporations have risen as the new rulers of the world. The gifted, though still rare in number, are now intermingled with the general populace. The top 0.5% wealthiest people control everything, and the majority of the population live as wage slaves in order to survive in their neon metropolises. There are some, however, that rebel against this new regime...
