In your former life, you had a taste of stardom, though you are now in the waning period of that fame. Whether you were an influencer, a famous musician, or a game streamer, you were once well-known among a good portion of the populace. How did you rise to fame, and more importantly, how did you fall?

**Skill Proficiencies:** Performance, plus your choice of Persuasion or Deception  
**Tool Proficiencies:** One type of musical instrument  
**Languages:** One of your choice  
**Equipment:** A set of fine clothes, a smartphone, a [[Currency|cryptostick]] with 1500 cp

## Feature: Conspicuous

People may recognize you if they have interest in topics related to your claim to fame, though they may react positively or negatively. You can always pull strings to get invitations to parties, conferences, and other social events. You have a following on various social media platforms that will help broadcast gossip, rumors, or scandals.
