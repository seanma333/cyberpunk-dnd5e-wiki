Most people follow the law in this world, continuing the constant struggle of climbing the social ladder to one day rise from their current status. Fools. You know that the game of law is rigged, so why play that game at all? You're a part of the vast underworld in the city, one where the only law is the law of the street. Were you a street thug, a part of an organized crime syndicate, or a member of one of the crime families that rule the underworld? Did you end up striking off on your own, or do you still maintain your connections?

**Skill Proficiencies:** Pick two of Athletics, Deception, Intimidation, and Stealth  
**Tool Proficiencies:** Thieves' Tools  
**Languages:** One of your choice  
**Equipment:** A set of common clothes, thieves' tools, a handgun, and a [[Currency|cryptostick]] with 500 cp

## Feature: Underworld Connection

You are in frequent contact with people in the criminal underworld. This connection comes in the form of a contact in any part of the city, a person who provides information about the people and places of the local area, as well as potential places to buy illegal goods.
