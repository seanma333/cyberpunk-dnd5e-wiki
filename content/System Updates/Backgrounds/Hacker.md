You understand that information is the newest form of wealth in this world, and your skills make you the newest form of thief. You are a hacker: a master of technology and cybersecurity. Some "white-hats" use their skills to work with clients (including the megacorps), breaking systems so they can be fixed before they are broken into by "black-hats", who work to steal from said clients. Which hat do you wear? More importantly, why did you leave your hacking station to roam the streets?

**Skill Proficiencies:** Investigation, plus your choice of Deception or Stealth  
**Tool Proficiencies:** [[Hacking|Hacker's Tools]] and one type of gaming set  
**Languages:** None  
**Equipment:** A set of common clothes, a smartphone, Hacker's Tools (basic), a universal cable, a [[Gear|wireless transceiver]], and a [[Currency|cryptostick]] with 500 cp.

## Feature: Hacker Central

You know the hacker lingo and slang, and you fit right into hacker culture. You also have access to a secure digital underground network for hackers, which you can use to contact for help (though it might not be cheap). The network also serves as an online marketplace where you can buy and sell valuable and potentially illegal data.
