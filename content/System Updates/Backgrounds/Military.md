Whether a state-run operation, or private armies employed by the megacorporations, those with a military background know that the concerns of those on the streets only exist because there are streets to be concerned about. You've trained since you were young, studied the history of war, learned basic survival techniques, including how to stay alive on the battlefield. You are not a civilian, you are a soldier... or at least, you were. What was it that made you leave?

**Skill Proficiencies:** Athletics, your choice of History or Intimidation  
**Tool Proficiencies:** Two of Vehicles (land), Vehicles (water), or Vehicles (air)  
**Languages:** None  
**Equipment:** An insignia of rank, an old uniform from your unit, a set of common clothes, a handgun, and a [[Currency|cryptostick]] with 1000 cp.

## Feature: Military Rank

You have a military rank from your career as a soldier. Soldiers loyal to your former military organization still recognize your authority and influence, and they defer to you if they are of a lower rank. You know your way around military etiquette, and so long that people think you are an active member, you can invoke your rank to exert influence over other soldiers and requisition simple equipment or [[Vehicles|vehicles]] for temporary use. You can also usually gain access to friendly military bases where your rank is recognized.
