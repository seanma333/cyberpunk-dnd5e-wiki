The world has changed so much, but this has not stopped the flow of magic. For you, technology is a crutch that holds up those who cannot fathom the depth of the mystical arts, and you know that there is a reason the knowledge and wisdom of the old world has lasted for so long. Some mystics prefer solitude, some join with each other to study or practice the old ways, and some form secret societies that aim to topple the new technocracy that has emerged in the world. What path do you follow?

**Skill Proficiencies:** Two of Arcana, Nature, or Religion  
**Tool Proficiencies:** None  
**Languages:** Two of your choice  
**Equipment:** A set of common clothes, a mystical item (an arcane focus, a druidic focus, or a holy symbol), a set of ink pens, a notebook, and a [[Currency|cryptostick]] with 1000 cp.

## Feature: Scholar of Magic

You can easily identify signs of recent magic usage. When you attempt to recall any lore regarding magic or the divine, even if you do not know that information, you often know where to obtain it. This usually comes from a library or university, or from a fellow knowledgeable mystic. Furthermore, you can always find a dealer of spell components and other arcane goods.
