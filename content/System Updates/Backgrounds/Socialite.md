While most toil in the dirty streets, you knew a life above all that. Your life was one of luxury: you attended many high end social events, and you spent a good part of your life learning to navigate the treacherous waters of upper class etiquette. Perhaps you were born into wealth, or perhaps you or your family found a way to gain it. Regardless, you have always known that in the world of the rich, it only takes a few mistakes before you are thrown out of the ivory tower and onto the streets below. So, what were your mistakes?

**Skill Proficiencies:** Persuasion, plus your choice of Deception or Insight  
**Tool Proficiencies:** None  
**Languages:** Two of your choice  
**Equipment:** A set of fine clothes, a signet ring with a symbol of your family, a smartphone, a [[Currency|cryptostick]] containing 2500 cp

## Feature: Social Standing

You know how to act around the upper class in order to blend in. You are easily accepted to high society functions as a member of the elite: those of the upper class will treat you as if you are of their social standing, though past connections or mistakes may still get in the way of your goals. You also know how to gain an audience with some members of the upper class.
