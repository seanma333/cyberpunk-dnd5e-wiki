Modern society's continued existence is dependent on the "essential worker". At least, that is what you've always been told by people far more privileged than you. Packed into cubicles and behind service counters, you engaged in a life-or-death struggle with boredom, impossible deadlines, impatient customers, and sadistic supervisors. You would trek home on sketchy subways and vandalized buses to your tiny apartment, barraged with endless attention-grabbing advertisements along the way. The job was soul-crushing, but it was the only thing keeping you off the streets. That is, until you no longer had it.

**Skill Proficiencies:** Choose two from among Deception, Persuasion, Perception, Sleight of Hand, Stealth, or Survival  
**Tool Proficiencies:** One tool of your choice  
**Languages:** One of your choice  
**Equipment:** A set of common clothes, a cheap employee of the month award, a set of artisan's tools, and a [[Currency|cryptostick]] with 500 cp

## Feature: Urban Survivor

You know the cheapest thrift stores, pawn shops, restaurants, and other stores where you can find discounts on everyday goods. You’re good at finding coupon codes and cut-rate prices for online shopping, event tickets, and public transportation. Additionally, you can make meals even when resources are very limited, such as from a fridge containing only condiments.
