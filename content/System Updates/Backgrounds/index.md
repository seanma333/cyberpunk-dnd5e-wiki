---
title: "Backgrounds"
---

Backgrounds in 5e are key to making a character feel like they belong in a setting. The core 5e backgrounds are pretty flexible when it comes to setting and flavor, but for the most part they do not belong in a Cyberpunk setting. As such, I've created backgrounds more fitting for Cyberpunk.
