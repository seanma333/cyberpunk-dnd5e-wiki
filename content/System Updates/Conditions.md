The cyberpunk world brings new hazards along with its new technology. The following conditions are added to those found in the *Player's Handbook*.

## Burning

A burning creature or object is on fire. Burning is measured in three levels. An effect that causes a target to start burning gives it one or more levels of burning, as specified in the effect, to a maximum of 3 levels.

| Level | Fire Damage per Turn |
| :---- | :---- |
| 1 | 1d6 |
| 2 | 2d6 |
| 3 | 3d6 |

- At the start of each of its turns, a burning creature takes 1d6 fire damage for each level of burning it has. A creature that is grappling a burning creature, or carrying a burning object, takes the same amount of fire damage at the start of each of its turns.
- A burning creature or object sheds bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet.
- A creature can use its action to put out the flames on itself, or on a creature or object within 5 feet of it, ending all levels of burning on that target. Burning also ends if a gallon of water is splashed on the target, if the target is submerged in water, or if the flames are extinguished by an effect such as a [[Gear|Fire Extinguisher]].
- A creature that is immune to fire damage can't gain levels of burning.
- A burning object or [[Vehicles|vehicle]] takes its burning damage on initiative count 0 (losing initiative ties).

## Irradiated

Certain areas, objects, and creatures are irradiated, and touching or standing near them can be harmful to a creature's health. Irradiated is measured in six levels. An effect can give a creature one or more levels of irradiated, as specified in the effect's description. If an already irradiated creature suffers another effect that causes irradiation, its current level of irradiated increases by the amount specified in the effect.

A creature suffers the effects of its current level of irradiated as well as all lower levels. A creature that is immune to poison damage is immune to the irradiated condition.

| Level | Effect | Cure |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | When the creature makes an ability check, it rolls a d4 and subtracts the number rolled from the check. | Long rest, *lesser restoration* |
| 2 | When the creature makes an attack roll or a saving throw, it rolls a d4 and subtracts the number rolled from the roll. | Long rest, *lesser restoration* |
| 3 | The creature can't take reactions. | *Lesser restoration* |
| 4 | At the start of its first turn in any combat, the creature must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw, or it spends its action that turn retching and reeling. | *Greater restoration* |
| 5 | When another creature spends a total of 1 hour within 30 feet of the irradiated creature, that creature must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 level of irradiated, to a maximum of level 5. | *Greater restoration* |
| 6 | The creature dies. For 1 week, any creature that spends a total of 1 hour within 30 feet of its corpse must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or gain 1 level of irradiated. | *Raise dead* or similar magic |

A creature's level of irradiated is reduced by 1 when it finishes a [[Rest and Recovery#Long Rests|long rest]], but only if its level is 1 or 2. A spell listed in the Cure column for the creature's current level also reduces its level by 1. All effects of irradiation end when a creature's level is reduced below 1.

### Avoiding Radiation

When an effect lists an amount of time that a creature can spend within a certain distance of a radioactive source before having to make a saving throw, the radiation is spreading through the air. This radiation is blocked by 1 foot of stone, 1 inch of common metal, a thin sheet of lead, or 3 feet of wood or dirt. It's also blocked by a [[Gear|Hazmat Suit]], which counts as a thin sheet of lead.

Attacks and creature abilities that force a saving throw against irradiation are harder to avoid. A creature wearing a Hazmat Suit has advantage on the saving throw, but the suit doesn't automatically protect it.
