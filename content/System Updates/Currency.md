aliases:

---

The main unit of currency in the game is a universally accepted decentralized cryptocurrency called **cryptopoints** (abbreviated **cp**, and colloquially shortened to **crips**). These can be stored in an owner's bank account which is commonly accessible in machines based on biometrics. They can also be stored on **crypto sticks** (colloquially shortened to **cripsticks**) to be transferred, or directly on any smartphone without a bank account. All purchases accept this currency. For the sake of comparison to real world currency, a cryptopoint is about the equivalent to a dollar.

For ease of 5e conversion, a cryptopoint is the equivalent of a **copper piece** in terms of value.

Since it is a digital currency, cryptopoints have no physical weight.
