*(Note: This replaces the Armblade item from Eberron - Rising from the Last War. This version does not require the wielder to be Warforged.)*

You replace your arm with a prosthetic one that allows you to graft a melee weapon to it, becoming inseparable from you as long as it is attached to your body. You may graft any one-handed melee weapon to the arm, but replacing it requires surgery. Higher tier versions are made of stronger material, and allow you to graft more powerful magic weapons.

**Tier 1.** You may graft a non-magical one-handed melee weapon to your arm. As a bonus action, you can retract the weapon into your forearm or extend it from there. While it is extended, you can use the weapon as if you were holding it, and you can't use that hand for other purposes.

**Tier 2.** Previous tier applies, but you may instead graft a common, uncommon, or rare magic one-handed melee weapon. If the weapon requires attunement, you must attune to it using a different attunement slot from the arm. If the weapon is non-magical, it counts as magical for purposes of overcoming damage resistances and immunities. If the weapon does not already have a bonus to attack and damage rolls, it gets +1 to attack and damage rolls.

**Tier 3.** Previous tier applies, but you may instead graft a common, uncommon, rare, or very rare magic one-handed melee weapon, and the bonus to attack and damage rolls becomes +2.

**Tier 4.** Previous tier applies, but you may graft a magic one-handed melee weapon of any rarity, and the bonus to attack and damage rolls becomes +3.
