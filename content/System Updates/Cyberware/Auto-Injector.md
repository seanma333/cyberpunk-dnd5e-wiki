A compact reservoir and injection system is implanted in your forearm, connected directly to your bloodstream. It stores [[Drugs|drugs]] out of sight and delivers them in precisely measured doses the moment you need them.

**Tier 1.** This [[System Updates/Cyberware/index|cyberware]] can hold up to 3 doses of drugs, in any combination. Loading a dose into it takes an action. The doses are hidden inside your body and can't be found by a pat-down or a scan unless the searcher knows to look for the injector.

When you roll initiative, you can inject yourself with any number of loaded doses of a single drug (no action required). You can also do so as a bonus action on your turn.

Because the injector measures each dose precisely, you have advantage on [[Drugs#Overdosing|overdosing saving throws]] triggered by drugs you inject with it.
