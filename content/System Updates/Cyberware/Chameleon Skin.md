This [[System Updates/Cyberware/index|cyberware]] allows your skin to expel clouds of nanoparticles that refract light away from you, making you seem invisible to the casual observer. At higher tiers, the nanoparticles can follow and mask you in fast-paced combat.

**Tier 2.** As an action, you can turn invisible. Anything you are wearing or carrying is invisible with you. The cyberware can keep you invisible for up to 10 minutes, all at once or in several shorter periods, each using a minimum of 1 minute of the duration. You can end the invisibility early as a bonus action. The cyberware regains all of its duration daily at dawn.

The invisibility ends early if you attack or cast a spell. It also ends early if you take damage or start your turn in an area with strong wind; when it ends this way, you become visible, and the cyberware doesn't work again until the end of your next turn.

**Tier 3.** Previous tier applies, except attacking or casting a spell no longer ends the invisibility.

**Tier 4.** Previous tier applies, except you can turn invisible as an action or a bonus action, the cyberware can keep you invisible for up to 1 hour, and taking damage or strong wind no longer ends the invisibility.
