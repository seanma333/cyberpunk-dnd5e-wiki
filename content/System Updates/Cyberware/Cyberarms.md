Your arms are replaced with armored prosthetics, from cheap models off the street to top-of-the-line corporate designs. Reinforced knuckles and servo-driven joints put the force of a hydraulic press behind every punch. If you are missing one or both arms, this [[System Updates/Cyberware/index|cyberware]] replaces them. At tier 3, a piston built into each forearm can fire on impact.

**Tier 1.** When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike made with your cyberarms, it takes an extra 1d4 bludgeoning damage.

**Tier 2.** Previous tier applies. Your unarmed strikes made with your cyberarms count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage, and you gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with them.

**Tier 3.** Previous tiers apply, except the bonus to attack and damage rolls becomes +2. In addition, your cyberarms have 5 charges. When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike made with your cyberarms, you can expend 1 charge to deal an extra 2d6 thunder damage to it and push it up to 10 feet away from you. The cyberarms regain 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.

**Tier 4.** Previous tiers apply, except the bonus to attack and damage rolls becomes +3, and when you expend a charge, the creature takes an extra 4d6 thunder damage instead of 2d6, is pushed up to 20 feet away from you, and is knocked prone.
