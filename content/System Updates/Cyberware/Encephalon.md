Rewires certain connections between your frontal lobe and hippocampus, allowing for more efficient storage, transfer, and retrieval of both short-term and long-term memories. Also includes a bioware sample of gray matter installation that comes with built in knowledge. At tier 3, a helpful artificial intelligence is installed into your brain.

**Tier 1.** You gain a +2 bonus to Intelligence ability checks and Intelligence saving throws. While you have this [[System Updates/Cyberware/index|cyberware]] installed, you gain proficiency with one skill. When you roll initiative, you can make an Intelligence check instead of a Dexterity check.

When you make an ability check or saving throw not using your Intelligence modifier, you can use your Intelligence modifier instead of the original ability. You can't use this ability again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Tier 3.** Previous tier applies, except you gain a +4 bonus to Intelligence ability checks and Intelligence saving throws instead of a +2.

Additionally, you can activate your AI's prediction algorithm as a bonus action. When you use this ability, and as a bonus action every round for 1 minute, you can choose to either gain advantage on your attack rolls and ability checks or to impose disadvantage on attack rolls against you until the start of your next turn. You can't use this ability again until you finish a long rest.
