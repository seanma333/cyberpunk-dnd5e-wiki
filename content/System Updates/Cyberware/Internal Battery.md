You install a battery into your body that can be used to power your legs or shock your foes. At tier 3, it automatically detects erratic heartbeats and delivers a defibrillating shock.

**Tier 2.** This [[System Updates/Cyberware/index|cyberware]] has 10 charges. You can spend 2 charges to Dash as a bonus action. When you hit a target with a melee attack, you can spend up to 3 charges and deal 1d6 lightning damage per charge. If you take a minimum of 4 lightning damage in a turn, you can regain a charge. If you spend 10 minutes connected to a power source, such as a wall outlet or a vehicle battery, you regain charges equal to half this cyberware's maximum number of charges. Once you do so, you can't do it again until you finish a short or long rest. You regain 1d8+2 charges over the course of a long rest.

**Tier 3.** Previous tier applies, except this cyberware has 20 charges, and it regains 2d8+4 charges over the course of a long rest.

Additionally, when you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can expend all charges and drop to 1 hit point instead so long as this cyberware has at least 1 charge. If this feature was triggered by a melee attack, you can use your reaction to deal 1d6 lightning damage per charge expended, to a maximum of 10d6, to the attacker. You can't use this feature again until you finish a long rest.
