You have small jets installed in your torso and limbs, which allow you to fly. Your battery for these jets is limited, but your body naturally recharges it using your body heat and blood circulation. At tier 3, the jets are fitted with stabilizers that let you hover in place and hold steady when hit.

**Tier 2.** You have a flying speed equal to your walking speed. You can fly for up to 4 hours, all at once or in several shorter flights, each using a minimum of 1 minute of the duration. If you are flying when the duration expires, or if you are knocked prone, you fall. You regain 2 hours of flying capability for every 12 hours you aren't flying.

As a bonus action, you can use 1 hour of your flying capability to double your flying speed for 1 minute. You can't use this ability again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Tier 3.** Previous tier applies. You can hover, and you have advantage on any ability check or saving throw you make to avoid being knocked prone while flying. Additionally, doubling your flying speed no longer uses any of your flying capability.
