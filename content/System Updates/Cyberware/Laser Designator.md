title: "Laser Designator"
tags:
  - cyberware
---

A targeting laser is installed in one of your eyes, letting you paint enemies for your allies' weapons. The laser is invisible to the naked eye, but your allies' optics and targeting systems pick it up instantly.

**Tier 1.** As a bonus action, you can paint a creature you can see within 120 feet of you. Until the start of your next turn, attack rolls against the painted creature have a +2 bonus, and ranged attacks against it ignore half cover. Only one creature can be painted by this [[System Updates/Cyberware/index|cyberware]] at a time, and multiple designators painting the same creature don't increase the bonus. Once you use this ability, you can't use it again until the end of your next turn.
