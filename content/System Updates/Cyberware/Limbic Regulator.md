title: Limbic Regulator
tags:
  - cyberware
---

A cluster of specialized nervous tissue is grafted into your brainstem and wired into your limbic system, where it amplifies the body's own pain gate and dampens the panic signals that flood the brain under stress. You stay calm when you should be screaming, and that clarity lets you read a room, spot a threat, or see through a lie while everyone around you is rattled, though with your body's warning signals muted, you can't always tell how badly you're hurt. At tier 3, the tissue also shields your mind from fear and outside influence.

**Tier 1.** You gain a +2 bonus to Wisdom ability checks and Wisdom saving throws, and you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened. While you have half your hit points or fewer, you gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus at the start of each of your turns. Because you can't feel much through this [[System Updates/Cyberware/index|cyberware]], you have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on touch.

**Tier 3.** Previous tier applies, except you gain a +4 bonus to Wisdom ability checks and Wisdom saving throws instead of a +2, you are immune to the charmed and frightened conditions, and the temporary hit points you gain equal twice your proficiency bonus.

Additionally, when you fail an Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma saving throw, you can use your reaction to succeed instead. You can't use this ability again until you finish a short or long rest.
