tags:

Your bone marrow is modified to mass-produce engineered platelets, which flood your bloodstream the moment you are injured and seal wounds almost as soon as they open. At tier 3, the platelets are fast enough to patch you up in the middle of a firefight as if the wound was never there.

**Tier 1.** When you take damage, you can use your reaction to gain temporary hit points equal to half the damage taken (rounded down). These temporary hit points last for 1 hour. You can use this ability a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

**Tier 3.** Previous tier applies, except the temporary hit points you gain equal the full damage taken.
