Designed for those without the physical knack for aiming but who still want to rain lead. Sensors in your eyes and electrodes in your brain are linked to a gun with a Smartgun system to assist with aiming, allowing you to instead focus on muscular compensation for recoil, muzzle rise, and target movement. Advanced versions of the interface allow you to spot weaknesses in your target's defenses (including magical defenses) and bypass them.

**Tier 1.** At the end of a short or long rest, you may link the augment to one [[Weapons#Firearms|firearm]] with a [[Weapon Upgrades#Smartgun System|Smartgun System]] installed. When you attack with the linked weapon, you may use your Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma, instead of Dexterity, for ranged attack rolls. You may only link to one Smartgun System at a time.

**Tier 2.** Previous tier applies. In addition, attacks with the linked weapon count as magical for purposes of overcoming damage resistances and immunities. If the linked weapon is not a magic weapon with a bonus to attack and damage rolls, it gains a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

**Tier 3.** Previous tier applies, except the bonus to attack and damage rolls becomes +2.

**Tier 4.** Previous tier applies, except the bonus to attack and damage rolls becomes +3.
