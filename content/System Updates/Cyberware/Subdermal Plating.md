title: "Subdermal Plating"
tags:
  - cyberware
---

Armored plates sculpted to fit your body are fused with your skin. When these are installed, they can be customized for appearance and stylistic purposes, potentially making them difficult to detect or immediately obvious at a glance. At tier 2, the plating becomes reactive, and you can cause it to harden against certain attacks. At tier 3, the plating is bonded to a cross-dermal sheath of cultured tissue that knits wounds closed in seconds.

**Tier 1.** You gain a +1 bonus to AC. You also gain +1 to Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws.

**Tier 2.** Previous tier applies. In addition, when you take damage of a type other than psychic damage, you can use your reaction to gain resistance to the damage type for 1 minute. You can't use this ability again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Tier 3.** Previous tiers apply. In addition, at the start of each of your turns, you regain hit points equal to your proficiency bonus if you have at least 1 hit point.
