tags:

A bioengineered thyroid gland replaces your own, flooding your body with hormones that accelerate your metabolism and push every physical system past its natural peak. Your muscles, reflexes, and endurance all surpass what any unmodified body can achieve, but your body burns through fuel at a ferocious rate.

**Tier 4.** Your Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution scores each increase by 2, to a maximum of 22. In addition, you need to eat twice as much food each day as normal.
