Cyberpunk settings often deal with what humanity is, and the replacement of organic body parts with machine parts are an allegory for what is the measure of a person's humanity. For game purposes, cybernetic enhancements (known colloquially in the world as **cyberware**) give special abilities to people through augmenting their bodies. Cyberware is incredibly useful, but it is expensive, and most cyberware comes with different tiers: the higher the tier, the more powerful the cyberware..

## Installing Cyberware

Having your body altered, often having whole limbs or organs replaced, takes a toll on the body and its essence. The surgical process of replacing natural body parts requires the body to form a bond with the cyberware in order to not reject the unnatural process it is going through.

For game purposes, installing cyberware takes up an attunement slot for a magic item, of which you would normally have 3. You can only end an attunement to a cyberware by having it surgically removed. You can only attune to one of each specific cyberware, and you cannot attune to two different tiers of the same cyberware. The cyberware tier determines the cost of the surgery, as outlined below.

Cyberware can only be installed or extracted safely by a cybersurgeon or by someone with a Cybersurgery Kit, in order to not cause complications to the receiver or damage to the cyberware. If someone uses a Cybersurgery Kit to install or extract cyberware, the tool kit holder must make a tool check with the Cybersurgery Kit, using either Dexterity or Intelligence as the ability for it. The DC for the check is outlined below.

### Surgery Fees

| Surgery Type | Fee | Tool Check DC |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Cyberware Tier 1 | 1,000 cp | 15 |
| Cyberware Tier 2 | 5,000 cp | 20 |
| Cyberware Tier 3 | 25,000 cp | 25 |
| Cyberware Tier 4 | 125,000 cp | 30 |

## Upgrading Cyberware

Some cyberware have multiple tiers, indicating more advanced technology for higher tiers. You can upgrade from a lower tier cyberware implant to a higher tier version for the cost of the surgery fee at the new tier and the difference between the cost of the higher tier version and the lower tier version.
