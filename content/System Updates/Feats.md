The following are new feats that can be taken by characters in the Cyberpunk Setting. Some of the feats replace existing feats in order to fit better with the new setting and rules; each one notes the feat it replaces.

## Combat Hacker

*Prerequisite: Proficiency with [[Hacking|Hacker's Tools]]*  
Your hacking skills have become instinctual to the point that you're able to break systems even in the middle of a firefight. You gain the following benefits:

* Increase your Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
* You may make tool checks using Hacker's Tools to interact with hackable objects that are not being worn or carried as a bonus action, instead of an action. You may not make such a tool check as an action on the same turn that you make one as a bonus action.  
* As an action, if you have Hacker's Tools and a wireless transceiver, you can target a [[Weapons#Firearms|firearm]], a worn headset, or a Construct with the Electronics property that you can see within 60 feet of you, and cause a different effect depending on the target. You cannot use this ability on the same target again until one minute has passed.  
  * If the target is a firearm, you cause the internal system to reboot, and the firearm becomes unusable until the start of your next turn.  
  * If the target is a worn headset, you overload the HUD on the headset, causing the wearer to be blinded until the start of your next turn.  
  * If the target is a Construct with the Electronics property, it must make a Constitution saving throw (DC is 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier). On a failure, you take control of the target's next turn. On a success, the construct is incapacitated until the start of your next turn.

## Cyberman

Your body becomes more acclimated to artificial body parts and enhancements. You gain the following benefits.

* You may attune to one [[System Updates/Cyberware/index|cyberware]] without using an attunement slot.  
* You may select this feat multiple times.

## Demolitionist

*Prerequisite: Proficiency with [[Tools|Demolitionist's Toolkit]]*  
You are a master at making things explode. You gain the following benefits:

* Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
* When you use or arm an [[Explosives|explosive]], you may add your proficiency bonus to all saving throw DCs of that explosive.  
* You gain the ability to quickly craft effective explosives. If you have a Demolitionist's Toolkit on hand during a short or long rest, you may craft one explosive up to a maximum tier of half your proficiency bonus (rounded down) by spending one hour crafting. This explosive is makeshift and will not work 24 hours after being completed, and this explosive cannot be sold.

## Getaway Driver

You have become almost supernaturally familiar with [[Vehicles|vehicles]] of all shapes and sizes, gaining the following benefits:

* Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
* You gain proficiency with all vehicles.  
* You can maintain control of a vehicle without having a free hand for the vehicle’s controls, though you must still be sitting in it.  
* When you are driving a vehicle and your vehicle or anyone inside the vehicle is hit with an attack, you can use your reaction to add your proficiency bonus to the AC of the target against that attack, potentially causing it to miss.  
* If a vehicle you are driving is subjected to an effect that allows it to make a Dexterity save to take only half damage, it takes no damage if it succeeds and only half damage if it fails.

## Gunslinger

*This feat replaces the Gunner feat from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything.*

You have a quick hand and keen eye when employing firearms, granting you the following benefits:

* Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
* You gain proficiency with all firearms.  
* Being within 5 feet of a hostile creature doesn’t impose disadvantage on your ranged attack rolls.  
* You do not need a free hand to reload a firearm.

## Rigger

*Prerequisite: Proficiency with Roboticist's Tools*  
Your skills with robotics are unparalleled, to the point where you are able to easily control multiple [[Drones|drones]] to aid your work. You gain the following benefits:

* You may control a number of drones up to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1) using a single [[Gear|Remote Controller]]. You may only do this for one remote controller at a time.  
* You may use the Remote Controller to control drones as a bonus action, rather than an action. You may not use the controller as an action on the same turn you use it as a bonus action. If you have a class feature that commands a companion as a bonus action (such as a Beast Master Ranger or a Battle Smith Artificer), you may control drones as part of the same bonus action you use to command a companion.  
* At the end of a short or long rest, if you have Roboticist's Tools on your person, you may modify a number of drones that you are carrying up to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1). You may choose a number of the following benefits equal to half your proficiency bonus (rounded up) to grant a modified drone. The benefits do not need to be the same for each drone you modify.  
  * The modified drone gains temporary HP equal to twice your character level.  
  * The modified drone gains a bonus to attack rolls equal to your proficiency bonus.  
  * The modified drone gets a bonus to armor class equal to your proficiency bonus.  
  * The modified drone gets a bonus to saving throws equal to your proficiency bonus.

## Street Samurai

You have rigorously trained your body and mind in the ways of melee combat, allowing you to unsheathe your weapons at incredible speeds and deflect attacks. You gain the following benefits:

* Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
* The first time you draw a melee weapon in combat, you gain advantage on the first attack with that weapon, and if that attack hits, it deals extra damage equal to 1d8 + your proficiency bonus.  
* When you make a melee weapon attack as part of the Attack action on your turn, you gain a +2 bonus to your AC against ranged attacks until the start of your next turn, as long as you are holding a melee weapon and are not incapacitated. You do not gain this benefit if you are wielding a shield.  
* If a creature within 5 feet of you makes a ranged attack, you may use your reaction to make a melee weapon attack against that creature.

## Tool Expert

You have honed your proficiency with particular tools, granting you the following benefits:

* Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
* You gain proficiency with one tool of your choice.  
* Choose one tool with which you have proficiency. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make with it. The tool you choose must be one that isn’t already benefiting from a feature that doubles your proficiency bonus.
