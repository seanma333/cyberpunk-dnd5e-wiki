## Land Vehicles

### Motorcycle

The motorcycle is an icon of the cyberpunk world: fast, loud, and nimble enough to weave through traffic that would stop anything else in its tracks. That speed comes at a price, since nothing stands between the rider and everything trying to kill them.

***Agile.*** The motorcycle can move through the space of a Large or larger vehicle as if it were difficult terrain, and it has advantage on Dexterity saving throws against [[Vehicles#Road Hazards|road hazards]].

### Car

The everyday workhorse of the city's streets, from beat-up sedans to sleek sports cars. A car has no special tricks, but it's cheap, reliable, and has plenty of room for [[Vehicles#Modding a Vehicle|mods]], making it the most common starting point for a custom ride.

***Covered.*** The vehicle has an enclosed cabin. Its riders have half cover against attacks from outside the vehicle, or three-quarters cover if they are Small or smaller. While the vehicle has hit points, its riders also have advantage on saving throws against spells that originate outside the vehicle, and take half damage from them (see [[Vehicles#Damage to Riders|Damage to Riders]]).

### Van

A boxy, reliable people-mover with a big cargo area, popular with delivery services, tradespeople, and lancer crews who need to move a team and their gear at the same time.

***Covered.*** The vehicle has an enclosed cabin. Its riders have half cover against attacks from outside the vehicle, or three-quarters cover if they are Small or smaller. While the vehicle has hit points, its riders also have advantage on saving throws against spells that originate outside the vehicle, and take half damage from them (see [[Vehicles#Damage to Riders|Damage to Riders]]).

***Cargo Hauler.*** Riders seated in the van's cargo area don't count as squeezing while the van is over its seat capacity.

### Truck

Built for hauling heavy loads, a truck trades speed and comfort for a heavy frame and a lot of raw power, which makes it the vehicle of choice for anyone who expects to drive through things rather than around them.

***Covered.*** The vehicle has an enclosed cabin. Its riders have half cover against attacks from outside the vehicle, or three-quarters cover if they are Small or smaller. While the vehicle has hit points, its riders also have advantage on saving throws against spells that originate outside the vehicle, and take half damage from them (see [[Vehicles#Damage to Riders|Damage to Riders]]).

***Heavy Hitter.*** When the truck [[Vehicles#Crashing|crashes]] into a target, it counts as one size larger when determining the crashing damage.

### Bus

A massive public transit vehicle that hauls dozens of passengers through the city's streets. Slow to start and slower to turn, but once it gets moving, almost nothing can stop it.

***Covered.*** The vehicle has an enclosed cabin. Its riders have half cover against attacks from outside the vehicle, or three-quarters cover if they are Small or smaller. While the vehicle has hit points, its riders also have advantage on saving throws against spells that originate outside the vehicle, and take half damage from them (see [[Vehicles#Damage to Riders|Damage to Riders]]).

***Unstoppable.*** When the bus crashes into a Large or smaller vehicle or object, it doesn't stop.

***Lumbering.*** During a [[Vehicles#High-Speed Chases|high-speed chase]], the bus can't take the Dash action unless it moved in a straight line on its previous turn.

## Water Vehicles

### Jet Ski

A small personal watercraft that skims across canals and harbors at high speed, perfect for slipping through narrow waterways where larger boats can't follow.

***Agile.*** The jet ski can move through the space of a Large or larger vehicle as if it were difficult terrain, and it has advantage on Dexterity saving throws against hazards on the water.

### Motorboat

A fast, covered boat used by harbor patrols, smugglers, and anyone else who needs to get across the water in a hurry.

***Covered.*** The vehicle has an enclosed cabin. Its riders have half cover against attacks from outside the vehicle, or three-quarters cover if they are Small or smaller. While the vehicle has hit points, its riders also have advantage on saving throws against spells that originate outside the vehicle, and take half damage from them (see [[Vehicles#Damage to Riders|Damage to Riders]]).

### Cruiser

A large cabin cruiser built for long trips on open water, complete with sleeping quarters below deck. Popular with the wealthy, and with anyone who needs to disappear offshore for a while.

***Covered.*** The vehicle has an enclosed cabin. Its riders have half cover against attacks from outside the vehicle, or three-quarters cover if they are Small or smaller. While the vehicle has hit points, its riders also have advantage on saving throws against spells that originate outside the vehicle, and take half damage from them (see [[Vehicles#Damage to Riders|Damage to Riders]]).

***Living Quarters.*** The cruiser's cabin has bunks, a galley, and a washroom. With the DM's approval, it can serve as a safe location for a [[Rest and Recovery#Long Rests|long rest]].

## Air Vehicles

### Helicopter

A rotorcraft that can take off and land almost anywhere, used by corporate executives, security forces, and news crews alike.

***Covered.*** The vehicle has an enclosed cabin. Its riders have half cover against attacks from outside the vehicle, or three-quarters cover if they are Small or smaller. While the vehicle has hit points, its riders also have advantage on saving throws against spells that originate outside the vehicle, and take half damage from them (see [[Vehicles#Damage to Riders|Damage to Riders]]).

***Hover.*** The helicopter can remain in the air without moving.

***Stable Flight.*** Whenever the helicopter makes an ability check or saving throw to avoid being knocked prone, it can treat a d20 roll of 7 or lower as an 8.

### Small Jet

A private jet that can cross the continent in hours. Its speed in the air is unmatched, but it needs a runway and plenty of open sky to use it.

***Covered.*** The vehicle has an enclosed cabin. Its riders have half cover against attacks from outside the vehicle, or three-quarters cover if they are Small or smaller. While the vehicle has hit points, its riders also have advantage on saving throws against spells that originate outside the vehicle, and take half damage from them (see [[Vehicles#Damage to Riders|Damage to Riders]]).

***Airspeed.*** While flying, the jet must move at least half its flying speed each turn, or it begins to fall.

***Runway.*** The jet needs at least 120 feet of straight, clear ground to take off or land.

### Floatplane

A propeller plane fitted with floats instead of wheels, able to take off from and land on any calm stretch of water. A favorite of smugglers who need to reach places without an airport.

***Covered.*** The vehicle has an enclosed cabin. Its riders have half cover against attacks from outside the vehicle, or three-quarters cover if they are Small or smaller. While the vehicle has hit points, its riders also have advantage on saving throws against spells that originate outside the vehicle, and take half damage from them (see [[Vehicles#Damage to Riders|Damage to Riders]]).

***Airspeed.*** While flying, the floatplane must move at least half its flying speed each turn, or it begins to fall.

***Water Landing.*** The floatplane can only take off from and land on water.
