Ease of customizing weapons is one of the reasons that technology has managed to match magic in the cyberpunk world. There are two major types of upgrades:
- **Attachments**: Items that can be attached to or detached from weapons. Attaching or detaching an attachment takes an action in combat. There is no limit to the number of attachments a weapon can have.
- **Modifications**: Items that permanently change a weapon. The number of modifications a weapon can have depends on the weapon, as listed in the Modifications column of the [[Weapons]] tables.

A weapon cannot have more than one of the same attachment or modification. A property gained from another modification counts toward a modification's requirements; for example, a longsword with [[#Lightweight Design]] gains the finesse property, so it can also take a [[#Recoil Hilt]].

A modification that requires a damage type (such as Arc Point, Mono-Edge, or Siegetek) only takes effect on attacks that deal that type of damage. A weapon that can deal more than one damage type, such as a glaive used with the Polearm Master feat, can take a modification for each type, using a separate modification slot for each.

## Arc Point

*Requirement: Melee weapon that deals piercing damage*  
The weapon's point houses a high-voltage capacitor that discharges into whatever it pierces.

Whenever you deal piercing damage with this weapon, you deal an additional 1d6 lightning damage.

- **Overload.** When you score a critical hit with this weapon that deals piercing damage, the target can't take reactions and has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of your next turn.
- **Armor-Piercing.** Piercing damage dealt by this weapon ignores resistance to piercing damage. In addition, you have advantage on attack rolls with this weapon that deal piercing damage against creatures with the Electronics feature.

## Ballistic Blade

*Requirement: Melee weapon with the heavy property*  
The weapon's broad blade or head is plated with layered ballistic alloy, turning it into a shield against gunfire for anyone strong enough to swing it into the line of fire.

While you are wielding this weapon and are proficient with it, when a creature you can see hits you with a ranged attack, you can use your reaction to add your proficiency bonus to your AC for that attack, potentially causing the attack to miss you.

## Bayonet

*Requirement: [[Weapons#Firearms|Firearm]] with the two-handed property*  
As an action, you can attach or detach a bayonet to your firearm. A firearm with a bayonet functions as a dagger for melee attack purposes, including opportunity attacks. You must use two hands when you make an attack with this weapon.

## Bullet Storm

*Requirement: Firearm with the automatic property*  
A firmware override disables the weapon's fire-rate limiter, letting it empty its entire magazine in a single, uncontrolled burst.

As an action, if this weapon has more than half of its reload capacity remaining, you can empty its magazine in a cone in front of you. The cone's length is equal to half the weapon's normal range. Each creature in the cone must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + the ability modifier you use for attack rolls with this weapon). A creature takes damage equal to twice the weapon's damage dice on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. The weapon's magazine is then empty and must be reloaded. Bullet Storm isn't an attack, so effects that modify attack or damage rolls don't apply.

Once you use Bullet Storm, you can't use it again until it recharges. At the start of each of your turns, roll a d6; on a 5 or 6, Bullet Storm recharges. It also recharges when you finish a short or long rest.

## Collapsible Frame

*Requirement: Melee weapon without the heavy property*  
The weapon's blade or haft is built from interlocking segments that fold down into a compact, unassuming package about the size of a flashlight.

While the weapon is collapsed, it can't be used to make attacks, and you have advantage on Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks made to conceal it on your person. Extending the weapon is part of drawing it, and collapsing it is part of stowing it, so neither requires anything more than the normal object interaction.

## Deadeye Stabilizer

*Requirement: Any ranged weapon*  
Weighted stabilizer rods and a gyroscopic dampener steady the weapon, rewarding a shooter with the patience to stay perfectly still.

If you haven't moved during this turn, you can brace before attacking. Until the end of the turn, when you hit with a ranged attack using this weapon, you can roll the weapon's damage dice twice and use either total. Bracing reduces your speed to 0 until the end of the current turn.

## Extended Magazine

*Requirement: Firearm with a reload capacity of 5 or more*  
As an action, you can attach or detach an extended magazine to a firearm. While it is attached, the firearm's reload capacity increases by half (rounded down). For example, an assault rifle's reload (20) becomes reload (30).

## High Impact Shots

*Requirement: Firearm with a Strength requirement*  
The weapon's firing power has been increased, allowing its targets to be blown backward by the velocity of the projectile. When you make a ranged weapon attack with this weapon against a creature that is within 30 feet of you, it deals an extra 1d6 damage, and you may push it up to 5 feet away if it is Large or smaller.

This extra power increases the recoil of the weapon and makes it more difficult to handle. The Strength requirement of the weapon is increased by 2.

## Kinetic Piston

*Requirement: Melee weapon*  
A pneumatic ram built into the weapon fires on impact, driving its target back with enough force to knock a grown adult off their feet or out from behind cover.

Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a melee attack using this weapon, you can attempt to shove that creature, without using an action or bonus action. Make the shove as normal: a Strength (Athletics) check contested by the target's Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. If you win, you either knock the target prone or push it 5 feet away from you.

## Lightweight Design

*Requirement: Any weapon*  
The weapon’s metal and wooden parts are replaced with plasteel, a tempered plastic. The weapon no longer sets off metal detectors and it is no longer affected by metal targeting effects. This weapon weighs half its normal weight and loses the heavy property if it had it. If the weapon didn't have the heavy property and is a melee weapon, it gains the finesse property.

A weapon with a modification that requires the heavy property, such as [[#Ballistic Blade]] or [[#Momentum Flywheel]], can't also have Lightweight Design.

## Magnetic Recall

*Requirement: Weapon with the thrown property*  
An electromagnetic tether links the weapon to a receiver worn on your wrist or palm.

Immediately after you make a ranged attack by throwing this weapon, it flies back to your hand, provided you have a free hand to catch it. If you don't, it lands at your feet.

## Mana Capacitor

*Requirement: Non-magical weapon*  
This weapon has been fitted with an embedded residuum crystal which can store magic for longer than normal. When this weapon is the target of a *magic weapon*, *elemental weapon*, or *holy weapon* spell, the spell lasts for 8 hours instead of its normal duration and doesn't require concentration.

A weapon with a Mana Capacitor cannot also have the [[#Spellbreaker]] modification.

## Mobile Shot

*Requirement: Ranged weapon without the two-handed property*  
A stabilized grip and recoil compensator let the wielder fire accurately while sprinting, sliding, or diving for cover.

When you take the Dash or Disengage action on your turn, you can use a bonus action to make one ranged attack with this weapon. If the weapon has the automatic property, you can use it for this attack.

## Momentum Flywheel

*Requirement: Melee weapon with the heavy property*  
A compact flywheel in the weapon's haft or head stores rotational energy, so that even a glancing swing carries enough force to hurt.

Once per turn, when you miss a creature with a melee attack using this weapon, the creature still takes damage equal to the ability modifier you used for the attack roll (minimum of 1). The damage is the same type as the weapon's damage.

## Mono-Edge

*Requirement: Melee weapon that deals slashing damage*  
Monomolecular edges, or "mono-edges", refer to blades that have been precision sharpened by lasers to the point where the cutting edge is measured in terms of molecules. In actuality, the term is more marketing than scientific, but the blades are still sharp enough to cause severe damage.

Whenever you deal slashing damage with this weapon, you deal an additional 1d8 slashing damage.

- **Severing Strike.** When you score a critical hit with this weapon that deals slashing damage, roll the Mono-Edge's extra damage die one additional time.
- **Cleave.** Once per turn, if you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with an attack using this weapon that deals slashing damage, you can immediately make another attack with the same weapon against a different creature within reach.

## Neurotoxin Feed

*Requirement: Any ranged weapon*  
A small reservoir in the weapon coats each round, bolt, or arrow with a fast-acting paralytic agent as it's fired.

Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a ranged attack using this weapon, its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. This has no effect on a creature that has resistance or immunity to poison damage, or immunity to the poisoned condition.

## Parry Module

*Requirement: Melee weapon*  
Gyroscopic counterweights in the weapon's grip snap it into a blocking position faster than human reflexes alone could manage.

While you are wielding this weapon and are proficient with it, when a creature you can see hits you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to add your proficiency bonus to your AC for that attack, potentially causing the attack to miss you.

## Payload Launcher

*Requirement: Light crossbow or heavy crossbow*  
A rail-mounted cradle slung beneath the crossbow's prod lets its powerful mechanical draw hurl an [[Explosives|explosive]] grenade in place of a bolt, delivering it far beyond the reach of a throwing arm.

When you would throw a grenade, you can instead launch it with this weapon. It takes the same action as throwing the grenade, but you can target a point up to the weapon's long range away instead of 60 feet. If the point is beyond the weapon's normal range, the grenade drifts off course: roll a d4, and it lands that many times 5 feet away from the point you chose, in a random direction. The grenade then takes effect as if it had been thrown to where it lands.

## Power Shot

*Requirement: Any firearm*  
This weapon has been modified to fire with extra precision and power. When you make an attack with this weapon, you may add a bonus to the damage roll equal to half of the ability modifier you used to attack with it (rounded up, minimum +1).

## Recoil Hilt

*Requirement: Melee weapon with the finesse property and without the two-handed property*  
Micro-servos in the grip snap the weapon back into position after every strike, letting a skilled duelist follow through faster than the eye can track.

When you take the Attack action and attack with this weapon while wielding it in one hand and holding nothing in your other hand, you can use a bonus action to make one more melee attack with it.

## Scope

*Requirement: Any ranged weapon*  
As an action, you can attach or detach a scope to a ranged weapon. You can use the scope as a bonus action. When you do so, your next attack roll with the weapon can be made at long range without disadvantage until the start of your next turn.

## Siegetek

*Requirement: Melee weapon that deals bludgeoning damage*  
This weapon emits hypersonic sound waves that can shatter objects it strikes.

Whenever you deal bludgeoning damage with this weapon, you deal an additional 1d4 thunder damage.

- **Sunder.** When you score a critical hit with this weapon to deal bludgeoning damage against a construct or a creature wearing armor, the armor is permanently damaged, lowering its AC by 1 until it is repaired.
- **Demolisher.** Objects, [[Vehicles|vehicles]], and constructs are vulnerable to the bludgeoning damage dealt by this weapon. This doesn't apply to the extra thunder damage.

## Silencer

*Requirement: Firearm that does not have the Scatter or Mounted property*  
As an action, you can attach or detach a silencer to a firearm that does not have the Scatter or Mounted properties. While the silencer is attached to a firearm, it significantly muffles the sound but dampens the velocity of the projectile. Ranged weapon attacks with a firearm with a silencer attached can only be heard from 40 feet away, but the firearm's normal range is halved.

## Silvered

*Requirement: Melee weapon or 20 pieces of ammunition*  
The weapon or ammunition gains the silvered property. (PHB 148)

## Sling

*Requirement: Any weapon*  
As an action, you can attach or detach a sling to a weapon. When you drop the weapon, you can instead choose to have it hang its sling on your body. Only one weapon can hang from a sling at once.

## Smartgun System

*Requirement: Any firearm*  
A weapon with a smartgun system installed can link with its user's [[Smartgun Augment]], allowing them to use the benefits of the augment when using this weapon.

## Spellbreaker

*Requirement: Non-magical weapon*  
Spellbreaker technology refers to a special kind of non-magical tech that bypasses the defenses of magic users. If you make an attack with a weapon with the Spellbreaker modification, it ignores AC bonuses, resistances, and immunities from spells (examples: Shield, Blade Ward, Mage Armor, and Stoneskin). Also, when a creature that is concentrating on a spell is damaged by a weapon attack using a spellbreaker weapon or ammunition, it has disadvantage on the saving throw that it makes to maintain its concentration. Attacks with a Spellbreaker weapon count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

Once a weapon is modified with spellbreaker tech, it cannot be enchanted or infused (such as through the Artificer's Item Infusions), or be used as a material component for a spell. A Spellbreaker weapon cannot also have the [[#Mana Capacitor]] modification.

## Thrust Assist

*Requirement: Melee weapon*  
Micro-thrusters mounted along the weapon's hilt or head ignite mid-swing, hurling its wielder across the battlefield behind it.

When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can fire the thrusters once before one of your attacks with this weapon: you move up to 10 feet in a straight line toward a creature you can see. This movement doesn't cost any of your speed and doesn't provoke opportunity attacks. You can't fire the thrusters while your speed is 0.

## Titanstring

*Requirement: Shortbow or longbow*  
The bow's limbs are replaced with carbon-composite laminates and a cam system rated for draw weights no unaugmented archer could pull.

When you hit with an attack using this weapon, you also add your Strength modifier to the damage roll (minimum of 0).

## Wide Sweep

*Requirement: Firearm with the Scatter property*  
When making an attack with this weapon against a creature within the normal range of the weapon, you may also target a second creature within 5 feet of the original target with the same attack roll. If the attack hits the second target, they take damage equal to half the damage of the attack.
