Ease of weapon modification is one of the reasons that technology has managed to match magic in the cyberpunk world. The following modifications can be applied to certain weapons (requirements are outlined in the table). Certain modifications are based on a multiplier of the cost of the weapon. A weapon cannot have the same modification more than once.  
Certain mods are detachable, which means that once applied, they can be unapplied. **A single weapon can have at most 2 mods that are not detachable.**

## Bayonet

*Requirement: [[Weapons#Firearms|Firearm]] with the two-handed property*  
As an action, you can attach or detach a bayonet to your firearm. A firearm with a bayonet functions as a dagger for melee attack purposes, including opportunity attacks. You must use two hands when you make an attack with this weapon.

## High Impact Shots

*Requirement: Firearm with a Strength requirement*  
The weapon's firing power has been increased, allowing its targets to be blown backward by the velocity of the projectile. When you make a ranged weapon attack with this weapon against a creature that is within 30 feet of you, it deals an extra 1d6 damage, and you may push it up to 5 feet away if it is Large or smaller.

This extra power increases the recoil of the weapon and makes it more difficult to handle. The Strength requirement of the weapon is increased by 2.

## Lightweight Design

The weapon’s metal and wooden parts are replaced with plasteel, a tempered plastic. The weapon no longer sets off metal detectors and it is no longer affected by metal targeting effects. This weapon weighs half its normal weight, and the heavy property is removed if it is present.

## Mono-Edge

*Requirement: Melee weapon that deals slashing damage*  
Monomolecular edges, or "mono-edges", refer to blades that have been precision sharpened by lasers to the point where the cutting edge is measured in terms of molecules. In actuality, the term is more marketing than scientific, but the blades are still sharp enough to cause severe damage.

Whenever you deal slashing damage with this weapon, you deal an additional 1d8 slashing damage. Once per turn, if you kill an enemy with an attack with this weapon, you may immediately make another attack with the same weapon against a different enemy within reach.

## Power Shot

*Requirement: Any firearm*  
This weapon has been modified to fire with extra precision and power. When you make an attack with this weapon, you may add your Dexterity modifier to the damage roll. If the weapon has a Strength requirement, you may add your Strength modifier instead.

## Scope

*Requirement: Any firearm*  
You attach a scope to your firearm. You can use the scope as a bonus action. When you do so, your next attack roll with the firearm can be made at long range without disadvantage until the start of your next turn.

## Siegetek

*Requirement: Melee weapon that deals bludgeoning damage*  
This weapon emits hypersonic sound waves that can shatter objects it strikes. Objects, [[Vehicles|vehicles]], and constructs are vulnerable to bludgeoning damage dealt by this weapon. When you score a critical hit with this weapon to deal bludgeoning damage against a construct or a creature wearing armor, the armor is permanently damaged, lowering its AC by 1 until it is repaired.

## Silencer

*Requirement: Firearm that does not have the Scatter or Mounted property*  
As an action, you can attach or detach a silencer to a firearm that does not have the Scatter or Mounted properties. While the silencer is attached to a firearm, it significantly muffles the sound but dampens the velocity of the projectile. Ranged weapon attacks with a firearm with a silencer attached can only be heard from 40 feet away, but the firearm's normal range is halved.

## Silvered

*Requirement: Melee weapon or 20 pieces of ammunition*  
The weapon or ammunition gains the silvered property. (PHB 148)

## Sling

*Requirement: Any weapon*  
As an action, you can attach or detach a sling to a weapon. When you drop the weapon, you can instead choose to have it hang its sling on your body. Only one weapon can hang from a sling at once.

## Smartgun System

*Requirement: Any firearm*  
A weapon with a smartgun system installed can link with its user's [[Smartgun Augment]], allowing them to use the benefits of the augment when using this weapon.

## Spellbreaker

*Requirement: Non-magical weapon*  
Spellbreaker technology refers to a special kind of non-magical tech that bypasses the defenses of magic users. If you make an attack with a weapon with the spellbreaker property, it ignores AC bonuses, resistances, and immunities from spells (examples: Shield, Blade Ward, Mage Armor, and Stoneskin). Also, when a creature that is concentrating on a spell is damaged by a weapon attack using a spellbreaker weapon or ammunition, it has disadvantage on the saving throw that it makes to maintain its concentration.

Once a weapon is modified with spellbreaker tech, it cannot be enchanted or infused (such as through the Artificer's Item Infusions), or be used as a material component for a spell.

## Wide Sweep

*Requirement: Firearm with the Scatter property*  
When making an attack with this weapon against a creature within the normal range of the weapon, you may also target a second creature within 5 feet of the original target with the same attack roll. If the attack hits the second target, they take damage equal to half the damage of the attack.
