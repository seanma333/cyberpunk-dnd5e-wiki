**All existing weapons from the *Player's Handbook* still exist in this world, at the same prices as before.** However, guns are commonplace in cyberpunk settings, so in addition to classic D&D weapons, firearms are widely available.

## Firearms

Firearms are the most prevalent weapon type in the cyberpunk world for their ease-of-use and mass production. Firearms are all ***ranged weapons**,* so the rules for firearms are the same as rules for ranged weapons, with the following differences:

* Unlike other weapons, **you do not add an ability modifier to the damage roll of a firearm unless explicitly stated.**  
* If you use the Attack action to attack with a firearm with the *light* property, you may use a bonus action to make an attack with a firearm with the *light* property in your off-hand.  
* When attacking a target that is underwater with a firearm, the target has resistance to the damage of the attack.  
* All firearms can be heard from 200 ft. away when fired.  
* Ammunition for firearms is unusable after being fired. The fired ammunition may be recovered as usual (for example, for forensic investigation), but the ammunition cannot be used again.

### Firearm Properties

There are additional properties for firearms in addition to weapon properties laid out for other weapons.

* **Automatic.** When you take the Attack action to make an attack with this weapon, or use a bonus action to make an off-hand attack with this weapon, you can choose to make two attacks instead with a -5 penalty to your attack rolls, and the normal and long ranges of the weapon are halved. You cannot move between these attacks.  
* **Foregrip.** This weapon can be used with one or two hands. If used in two hands, its normal and long ranges double.  
* **Mounted.** This weapon is normally used while attached to a tripod, [[Vehicles|vehicle]], or other bracing mount. While it is mounted, you can ignore the weapon's Strength requirement if it has one, and it can't be moved from its mount without unmounting it. You can mount or unmount this weapon as an action. It can only be used to make an attack while unmounted if held by a Medium or larger creature with a Strength score of at least 15.  
* **Reload.** This weapon can be used to make a number of attacks before it must be reloaded. If you are not proficient with the weapon, reloading it takes an action. If you are proficient, you can reload it as a bonus action. Some weapons require an action to reload even if you have proficiency. This is specified in the reload property.  
* **Scatter.** If you make an attack against a target that is within half this weapon’s normal range, you deal the damage value listed in parentheses instead of the weapon’s normal damage dice.  
* **Sighted.** This weapon has disadvantage on attack rolls made against targets within 20 feet.  
* **Strength.** Any creature using this weapon has disadvantage on attack rolls with it if its Strength score is less than the value of the strength requirement. Furthermore, you may use your Strength modifier instead of your Dexterity modifier to make ranged weapon attacks with this weapon.

### Firearm Proficiency

All characters gain some firearm proficiency. If a character has proficiency with **simple weapons**, they also have proficiency with **simple firearms**. Likewise, if a character has proficiency with **martial weapons**, they also have proficiency with **martial firearms**.

In addition to the simple and martial weapon proficiencies, certain classes gain additional proficiencies:

| Class | Additional Proficiencies |
| :---- | :---- |
| Artificer | All firearms |
| Bard | Assault rifle, magnum |
| Druid | Double-barrel shotgun, hunting rifle |
| Rogue | Magnum, sniper rifle |
| Sorcerer | Handgun, hunting rifle, machine pistol, revolver |
| Wizard | Handgun, hunting rifle, machine pistol, revolver |
