This section outlines the changes and additions to the general rules of 5th edition D&D for a futuristic Cyberpunk-like setting, but keeping to the fantasy roots. Magic exists, but for the common people of the world, it is supplanted by modern-style technology.

Anyone that has played 5th edition Dungeons & Dragons should be able to play with these rules without much help, since much thought and care was made to have the updates still fit within the design principles of 5th edition, adding minimal crunch to character building and game time.

## Setting Design

The setting that results from these changes is a blend of classic fantasy (keeping the D&D races and magic system), and futuristic cyberpunk (technology has been updated to fit with the modern sci-fi subgenre). Similar to games like Shadowrun or Cyberpunk 2020, the setting is meant to be a futuristic dystopia where the world is run by powerful megacorporations. However, the D&D setting is also populated by classic fantasy races who have long since had to adapt to the new world.

With this blending of genres, the majority of character creation and play does not change at all: there are no updates to character races, classes (apart from every class gaining some weapon proficiencies for firearms), attributes, or skills. This means you can, for the most part, use the same character sheet as you would for the base game. Spellcasting also works exactly as it did before. Instead, most of the changes to the game are either re-flavoring (armor, currency), items (new weapons, gear, and tools), or new sub-systems (hacking, cybernetics, vehicles). There are also custom cyberpunk-specific backgrounds to make the character fit into the world.
